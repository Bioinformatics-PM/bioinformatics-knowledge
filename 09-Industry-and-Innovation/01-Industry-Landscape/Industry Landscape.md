# Industry Landscape

> [!abstract]
> Who employs bioinformaticians and why: the sectors (public research, pharma, biotech, contract research, clinical labs, diagnostics, tool vendors, agritech), what each one sells, and the roles and competencies they hire for.

## Why it matters for bioinformatics

The same skill (say, variant calling) is a research method in an institute, a validated service in a clinical lab, a product feature at a tool vendor and a cost center at a contract research organization. Knowing the sectors explains the job titles, the constraints (regulation, deadlines, confidentiality) and which competencies to show. The ISCB competency framework was built from surveys of employers and training programs and is the outcome check for this curriculum.[^iscb][^bench]

## Learning path

### Stage 3 - Advanced (L3)

1. [[Public Research Institute]] (L3): know the public employers (EMBL-EBI, NCBI, national institutes, hospital research, core facilities, ELIXIR nodes) and what their bioinformaticians do.
2. [[Pharmaceutical Industry]] (L3): describe the pharma value chain (research, development, manufacturing, commercial) and where bioinformatics sits in R&D.
3. [[Biotechnology Industry]] (L3): distinguish biotech segments (therapeutics, industrial, agricultural) and platform from product companies.
4. [[Contract Research Organization]] (L3): explain outsourcing to CROs and CDMOs, and bioinformatics as a service business.
5. [[Clinical Laboratory]] (L3): describe hospital and private genomic diagnostic labs and the clinical bioinformatician's work under accreditation.
6. [[Diagnostics Industry]] (L3): distinguish test kit makers from laboratory service providers and the role of companion diagnostics.
7. [[Life Science Tools Industry]] (L3): map instrument makers (sequencers, mass spectrometers), reagent suppliers and software and cloud platforms.
8. [[Agricultural Biotechnology]] (L3): know how plant and animal breeding, genomic selection and crop genomics employ bioinformaticians.
9. [[Biotech Cluster]] (L3): explain why biotech concentrates around universities, hospitals and investors, with examples in Europe and the US.
10. [[Bioinformatics Role]] (L3): tell apart bioinformatician, bioinformatics engineer, computational biologist, research software engineer, clinical bioinformatician and data scientist.
11. [[Bioinformatics Competency Framework]] (L3): map your skills and Lab projects to the ISCB core competencies and find the gaps.[^iscb]

> [!tip]
> Read this subdomain once early in Stage 4 to choose a specialization, then again before applying for internships: item 11 is a checklist for a portfolio.
