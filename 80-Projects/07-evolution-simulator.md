# 07-evolution-simulator

> [!abstract]
> A virtual laboratory of population genetics: mutation, selection and drift across generations.

## Objective

Simulate populations with configurable size, genome length, mutation rate, selection pressure and initial allele frequency, and observe allele frequencies, diversity and fitness over time.

## Concepts required

- [[Natural Selection]]
- [[Genetic Drift]]
- [[Wright-Fisher Model]]
- [[Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium]]
- [[Fitness]]
- [[Allele Frequency]]
- [[Markov Chain]]
- [[Random Number Generation]]

## Four questions

| Lens | Answer |
|---|---|
| Biology: what is modeled? | Evolution by mutation, selection and drift. |
| Mathematics: which model or algorithm? | Stochastic processes: Wright-Fisher, Markov chains, fixation probabilities. |
| Computer science: how is it implemented efficiently? | Vectorized simulation, reproducible random seeds. |
| Product: how does it become a usable tool? | Dynamic charts of allele frequencies over generations. |
