# 06-mutation-lab

> [!abstract]
> An interactive laboratory showing how a mutation propagates from DNA to codon to amino acid to protein.

## Objective

Select a mutation and observe its molecular consequence: silent, missense, nonsense or frameshift.

## Concepts required

- [[Mutation]]
- [[Silent Mutation]]
- [[Missense Mutation]]
- [[Nonsense Mutation]]
- [[Frameshift Mutation]]
- [[Genetic Code]]
- [[Protein]]

## Four questions

| Lens | Answer |
|---|---|
| Biology: what is modeled? | Mutation classes and their molecular effect. |
| Mathematics: which model or algorithm? | Probability of each class under a uniform point-mutation model. |
| Computer science: how is it implemented efficiently? | Consequence prediction reusing 02 and 03. |
| Product: how does it become a usable tool? | A timeline from original to mutated protein. |

## Scope

Molecular consequence only. It is not a clinical prediction, and the tool says so.
