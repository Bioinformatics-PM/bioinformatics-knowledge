# Gibbs 1970 - The Diagram, a Method for Comparing Sequences

> [!abstract]
> The paper that introduced the "diagram", now called the dot plot: a two-dimensional matrix comparing two sequences position by position.

## Why this source

It is the origin of the dot plot, the simplest visual method of sequence comparison. Gibbs and McIntyre used it to derive similarity measures for classifying 25 cytochromes by their amino acid sequences, to detect repetitions within protein sequences, and to detect regions of possible base pairing in nucleic acids: the three classic uses still taught today.
