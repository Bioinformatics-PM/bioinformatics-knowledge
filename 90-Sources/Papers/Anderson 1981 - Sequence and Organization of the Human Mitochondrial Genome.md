# Anderson 1981 - Sequence and Organization of the Human Mitochondrial Genome

> [!abstract]
> The complete 16,569-base-pair sequence of the human mitochondrial genome, with the location of its rRNA, tRNA and protein-coding genes.

## Why this source

The first component of the human genome to be sequenced completely; Frederick Sanger is among the authors. The paper located the genes for the 12S and 16S ribosomal RNAs, 22 transfer RNAs, cytochrome c oxidase subunits I, II and III, ATPase subunit 6, cytochrome b and eight other predicted protein-coding genes, that is 13 protein-coding genes in all. It gives the reference numbers for an organelle genome.
