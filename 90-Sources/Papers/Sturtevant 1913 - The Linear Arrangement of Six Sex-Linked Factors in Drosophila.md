# Sturtevant 1913 - The Linear Arrangement of Six Sex-Linked Factors in Drosophila

> [!abstract]
> The paper that built the first genetic map: recombination between six sex-linked factors of *Drosophila* places them in a single linear order along the chromosome.

## Why this source

The founding paper of genetic mapping, written by an undergraduate in Thomas Hunt Morgan's laboratory. Its logic (recombination frequency as a distance, additivity of short intervals, double crossovers) is still the logic of every linkage map.

## Coverage

Citation: Sturtevant AH. "The linear arrangement of six sex-linked factors in Drosophila, as shown by their mode of association". *Journal of Experimental Zoology* 14:43-59 (1913).
