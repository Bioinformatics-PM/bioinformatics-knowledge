# Neuman 2004 - Optical Trapping

> [!abstract]
> The standard review of optical trapping instruments for biophysics: trap design, position detection and the calibration of trap stiffness and force.

## Why this source

Single-molecule papers cite it for how an optical trap is built and calibrated. It is the reference for turning the thermal motion of a trapped bead into a stiffness, the main biological use of the [[Equipartition Theorem]].

## Coverage

Citation: Neuman KC, Block SM. "Optical trapping". *Review of Scientific Instruments* 75(9):2787-2809 (2004). doi:10.1063/1.1785844.
