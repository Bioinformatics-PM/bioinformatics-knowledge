# Baltimore 1970 - RNA-Dependent DNA Polymerase in Virions of RNA Tumour Viruses

> [!abstract]
> One of two back-to-back papers of June 1970 reporting an enzyme, carried inside the particles of RNA tumour viruses, that makes DNA from an RNA template: reverse transcriptase.

## Why this source

The landmark of RNA → DNA information transfer. In the same issue of *Nature*, Howard Temin and Satoshi Mizutani reported the same kind of activity in Rous sarcoma virus particles; the activity was inhibited by ribonuclease, which pointed to an RNA template. The finding showed how an RNA virus can leave a DNA copy of itself in a cell, and it fits the "special transfers" of [[Crick 1970 - Central Dogma of Molecular Biology|Crick's 1970 statement of the central dogma]]. Baltimore shared the 1975 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Renato Dulbecco and Howard Temin.
