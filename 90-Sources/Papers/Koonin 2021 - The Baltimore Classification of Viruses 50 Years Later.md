# Koonin 2021 - The Baltimore Classification of Viruses 50 Years Later

> [!abstract]
> A review that revisits the Baltimore classes in the light of virus evolution: which classes share an origin, how they relate to the monophyletic realms of virus taxonomy, and why many possible schemes are not found in nature.

## Why this source

The bridge between the textbook classification and current evolutionary virology. It explains why a Baltimore class is a strategy and not always a lineage, which matters whenever viruses are compared or classified from sequence.
