# Gilbert 1986 - The RNA World

> [!abstract]
> The one-page Nature piece that named "the RNA world", an early stage of life in which RNA both stored genetic information and catalyzed reactions.

## Why this source

The discovery of catalytic RNA in the early 1980s showed that one molecule type could be both gene and enzyme. Gilbert drew the consequence: the earliest living systems may have used RNA alone, with proteins and DNA added later. Others had hinted that RNA preceded DNA; Gilbert proposed an RNA-only stage and gave it the name that stuck.
