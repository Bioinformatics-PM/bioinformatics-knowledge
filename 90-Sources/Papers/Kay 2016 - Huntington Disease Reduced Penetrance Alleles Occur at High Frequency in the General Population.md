# Kay 2016 - Huntington Disease Reduced Penetrance Alleles Occur at High Frequency in the General Population

> [!abstract]
> A population study showing that Huntington disease alleles with 36 or more CAG repeats are carried by about 1 in 400 people, and that the penetrance of 36 to 38 repeats is far lower in the general population than estimated from clinical cohorts.

## Why this source

A clean, quantitative case of incomplete penetrance and of ascertainment bias: the same alleles look much more dangerous in families seen in clinics than in unselected people. It is the reference example for penetrance in [[Genetic Disease]].

## Coverage

Citation: Kay et al. "Huntington disease reduced penetrance alleles occur at high frequency in the general population". *Neurology*, July 2016 issue, pages 282-288 (published online 22 June 2016).

## Caveats

- Penetrance estimates for rare repeat lengths rest on few carriers and on modelling; read the confidence intervals in the paper.
