# Monod 1949 - The Growth of Bacterial Cultures

> [!abstract]
> Monod's classic review of bacterial growth: the phases of a batch culture, the definition of the growth rate on a logarithmic scale, and the hyperbolic dependence of the exponential growth rate on the concentration of a limiting nutrient.

## Why this source

The origin of the quantitative treatment of bacterial growth that textbooks still teach, and of the "Monod equation" used in bioprocess and ecological models.

## Coverage

Citation: Monod J. "The growth of bacterial cultures". *Annual Review of Microbiology* 3:371-394 (1949).
