# Phillips 2018 - Membranes by the Numbers

> [!abstract]
> A chapter that reviews the parameters characterizing the mechanical, electrical and transport properties of membranes and checks their values with order-of-magnitude estimates.

## Why this source

Written by the first author of [[Physical Biology of the Cell (Phillips)]] in the same "by the numbers" style, it gives the measured values of membrane parameters next to the simple physical models that explain them. The electrical part is a short, citable treatment of the membrane as a capacitor.

## Coverage

Citation: Phillips R. "Membranes by the Numbers", in *Physics of Biological Membranes*, Bassereau P. and Sens P. (eds.), Springer, 2018. Preprint: arXiv:1703.02066.

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|

## How to use it

- L1 to L2: read the electrical section with [[Capacitance]] and [[Membrane Potential]].
- L3: use the whole chapter as a model of how to sanity-check membrane parameters ([[Order-of-Magnitude Estimation]]).
