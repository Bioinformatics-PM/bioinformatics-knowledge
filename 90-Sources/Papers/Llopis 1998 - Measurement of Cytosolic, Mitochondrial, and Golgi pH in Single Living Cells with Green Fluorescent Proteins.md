# Llopis 1998 - Measurement of Cytosolic, Mitochondrial, and Golgi pH in Single Living Cells with Green Fluorescent Proteins

> [!abstract]
> pH-sensitive variants of green fluorescent protein, targeted to the cytosol, the mitochondrial matrix or the Golgi, report the pH of each compartment in single living cells.

## Why this source

Textbooks give compartment pH values without saying how they were obtained. This paper shows a direct, non-invasive measurement in living cells, and its values (matrix about 8, Golgi about 6.6, cytosol about 7.3) are the ones textbooks and databases such as BioNumbers quote. It is also an early example of genetically encoded fluorescent sensors, a technique now common in cell biology.

## How to use it

- L1: take the values as the experimental basis of the compartment pH table in [[pH]].
- L2: read the method as an example of a calibrated fluorescent reporter ([[Fluorescence]]).

## Caveats

- Values are for specific cell types at rest; pH varies with cell type and metabolic state.
- Values and authors verified through search records of the paper (PNAS, BioNumbers entries); volume, issue and pages taken from the DOI record and not re-checked on the journal page.
