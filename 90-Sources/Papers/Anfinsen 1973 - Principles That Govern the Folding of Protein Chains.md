# Anfinsen 1973 - Principles That Govern the Folding of Protein Chains

> [!abstract]
> Anfinsen's synthesis of the ribonuclease refolding experiments and of the "thermodynamic hypothesis": the native structure of a protein is determined by its amino acid sequence, in a given environment, as the state of lowest free energy.

## Why this source

The founding statement of the principle every structure predictor relies on: if the sequence determines the fold, the fold can in principle be computed from the sequence. Reading it shows the experimental logic (denature, remove the denaturant, recover activity) behind a sentence that textbooks compress to "sequence determines structure".
