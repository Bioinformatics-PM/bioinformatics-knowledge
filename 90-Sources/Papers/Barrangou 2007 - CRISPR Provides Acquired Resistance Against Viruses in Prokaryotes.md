# Barrangou 2007 - CRISPR Provides Acquired Resistance Against Viruses in Prokaryotes

> [!abstract]
> The experimental demonstration that CRISPR arrays and their associated cas genes give bacteria a heritable, sequence-specific resistance to phages, acquired by inserting fragments of phage DNA as new spacers.

## Why this source

The landmark experiment showing that CRISPR arrays, repeats found in many prokaryotic genomes, act as an acquired, sequence-specific defense against viruses.

## Coverage

Citation: Barrangou R, Fremaux C, Deveau H, Richards M, Boyaval P, Moineau S, Romero DA, Horvath P. "CRISPR provides acquired resistance against viruses in prokaryotes". *Science* 315(5819):1709-1712 (March 2007). doi:10.1126/science.1138140.
