# Baltimore 1970 - An RNA Polymerase in the Virion of Vesicular Stomatitis Virus

> [!abstract]
> The discovery that particles of vesicular stomatitis virus, a negative-strand RNA virus, carry an RNA polymerase that copies the genome into complementary RNA, explaining why the naked genome is not infectious.

## Why this source

It established that a virus whose genome cannot serve as mRNA must bring its own polymerase inside the particle. The same reasoning, applied to every genome type, underlies the [[Baltimore Classification]].
