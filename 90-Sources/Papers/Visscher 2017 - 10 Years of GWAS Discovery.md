# Visscher 2017 - 10 Years of GWAS Discovery

> [!abstract]
> A review of the first ten years of genome-wide association studies: what they revealed about the genetic architecture of complex traits and diseases, their biology, and their translation toward therapeutics.

## Why this source

The standard overview of what association studies taught: complex traits and common diseases are highly polygenic. It gives the empirical ground for the complex side of [[Genetic Disease]] and a map of where [[Genome-Wide Association Study|GWAS]] results are used.

## Coverage

Citation: Visscher PM, Wray NR, Zhang Q, Sklar P, McCarthy MI, Brown MA, Yang J. "10 Years of GWAS Discovery: Biology, Function, and Translation". *American Journal of Human Genetics* 101(1):5-22 (2017). doi:10.1016/j.ajhg.2017.06.005. Free at PMC5501872.
