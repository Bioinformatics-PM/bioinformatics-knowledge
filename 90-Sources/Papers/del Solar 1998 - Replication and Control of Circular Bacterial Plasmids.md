# del Solar 1998 - Replication and Control of Circular Bacterial Plasmids

> [!abstract]
> A review of how circular bacterial plasmids replicate as autonomous elements and how they control their own replication.

## Why this source

The reference review behind the idea of a plasmid as a **replicon**: an origin plus the proteins and RNAs that start and regulate its replication. It organizes plasmid replication into stages and mechanisms, which is the background needed to understand copy number, incompatibility and host range.

## Coverage

Citation: del Solar G, Giraldo R, Ruiz-Echevarría MJ, Espinosa M, Díaz-Orejas R. "Replication and control of circular bacterial plasmids". *Microbiology and Molecular Biology Reviews* 62(2):434-464 (June 1998). PMID 9618448, free at PMC98921.

## Caveats

- A 1998 review: the molecular details have been refined since, and linear plasmids are outside its scope.
- Verified in this pass (web search): authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages, PMID and PMC record, and the scope, stages, mechanisms and host-range remark summarized above. The detailed sections on copy-number control and incompatibility were not read.
