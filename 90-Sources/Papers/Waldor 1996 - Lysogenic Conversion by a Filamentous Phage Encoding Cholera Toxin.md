# Waldor 1996 - Lysogenic Conversion by a Filamentous Phage Encoding Cholera Toxin

> [!abstract]
> The discovery that the genes for cholera toxin, the main virulence factor of *Vibrio cholerae*, are carried by a bacteriophage, CTXφ, which turns non-toxigenic strains into toxigenic ones.

## Why this source

The textbook case of **lysogenic conversion**: a phage that carries a virulence gene and changes what its host can do. It links phage biology to pathogen evolution and shows that the receptor a phage uses can itself be a virulence factor.
