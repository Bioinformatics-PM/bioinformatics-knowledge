# Lawrence 1998 - Molecular Archaeology of the Escherichia coli Genome

> [!abstract]
> A genome-wide estimate of how much of the *E. coli* chromosome arrived by horizontal gene transfer since its divergence from *Salmonella*, how fast, and how much of it was later lost.

## Why this source

The classic quantitative picture of horizontal gene transfer in one bacterial lineage. It shows that transferred genes can be recognized inside a single genome from their sequence characteristics, without any comparison of phylogenies, and it turns the result into numbers (genes, events, a rate) that make the mosaic nature of bacterial genomes concrete.

## How to use it

- L2: read the abstract and the summary table of transferred genes after [[Horizontal Gene Transfer]].
- L3: read the methods to see how sequence composition is used to recognize and date transferred genes, and compare with phylogenetic detection.

## Caveats

- The estimate depends on the compositional method: transfers from donors of similar composition, and old transfers whose composition has converged on the host's, are missed.
