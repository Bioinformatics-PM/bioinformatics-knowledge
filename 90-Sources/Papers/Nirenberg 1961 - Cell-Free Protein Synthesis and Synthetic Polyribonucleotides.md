# Nirenberg 1961 - Cell-Free Protein Synthesis and Synthetic Polyribonucleotides

> [!abstract]
> "The dependence of cell-free protein synthesis in E. coli upon naturally occurring or synthetic polyribonucleotides": the poly-U experiment that assigned the first codon of the genetic code.

## Why this source

By adding synthetic RNA to a cell-free *E. coli* extract, the authors showed that polyuridylic acid directs the synthesis of polyphenylalanine, so UUU codes for phenylalanine. This opened the experimental cracking of the [[Genetic Code|genetic code]].
