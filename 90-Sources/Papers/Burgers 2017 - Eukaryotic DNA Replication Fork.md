# Burgers 2017 - Eukaryotic DNA Replication Fork

> [!abstract]
> A review of how the eukaryotic replication fork is assembled and which enzymes synthesize and mature the leading and lagging strands.

## Why this source

Textbook descriptions of eukaryotic replication often lag behind research. This review by two leading groups summarizes the modern picture of the fork, with emphasis on the DNA polymerases and on the repair of lagging-strand discontinuities (Okazaki fragment maturation). It is the reference the vault uses to check which polymerase does what.
