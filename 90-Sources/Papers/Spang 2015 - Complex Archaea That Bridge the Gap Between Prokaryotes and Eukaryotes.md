# Spang 2015 - Complex Archaea That Bridge the Gap Between Prokaryotes and Eukaryotes

> [!abstract]
> Describes "Lokiarchaeota", a candidate archaeal phylum known from a genome reconstructed from metagenomic data, which branches with eukaryotes in phylogenomic analyses and encodes many eukaryotic signature proteins.

## Why this source

The landmark paper behind the idea that the eukaryotic host cell evolved from within the archaea. It is also a model of sequence-only discovery: an organism characterized from metagenomic sequence, without a culture.

## Coverage

Citation: Spang A, Saw JH, Jørgensen SL, et al., Ettema TJG. "Complex archaea that bridge the gap between prokaryotes and eukaryotes". *Nature* 521:173-179 (2015).

## Caveats

- A single composite genome from one environment; later work added related lineages and revised parts of the analysis. Read it for the result and the approach, and a current review for the present consensus.
- Metadata (journal, volume, pages, DOI, year) and the main claims verified in this pass; the author list is abbreviated.
