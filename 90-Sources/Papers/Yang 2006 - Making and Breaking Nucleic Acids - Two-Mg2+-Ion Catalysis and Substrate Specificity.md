# Yang 2006 - Making and Breaking Nucleic Acids - Two-Mg2+-Ion Catalysis and Substrate Specificity

> [!abstract]
> A review arguing that the two Mg²⁺ ions found at the active sites of all DNA and RNA polymerases, and of many nucleases and transposases, also make these enzymes specific for their substrates.

## Why this source

It states the two-metal-ion mechanism as a rule shared by the enzymes that make and break nucleic acids, and connects the coordination chemistry of Mg²⁺ (its sensitivity to ligand geometry and to electrostatics) to substrate recognition: the bridge between a general chemistry topic and polymerase biology.
