# Popejoy 2016 - Genomics Is Failing on Diversity

> [!abstract]
> A Nature Comment showing that participants in genome-wide association studies were overwhelmingly of European ancestry, and why that matters for science and health equity.

## Why this source

Counting samples in the GWAS Catalog, the authors found that in 2016 about 81% of participants were of European descent, and that people of African, Latin American and indigenous ancestry together made up less than 4%. Because genetic effects, allele frequencies and linkage patterns differ between populations, findings and risk scores built on one ancestry transfer poorly to others. The Comment made the diversity gap a mainstream issue.
