# Axelrod 1981 - Cell-Substrate Contacts Illuminated by Total Internal Reflection Fluorescence

> [!abstract]
> The founding paper of total internal reflection fluorescence (TIRF) microscopy: the evanescent wave of a totally internally reflected laser beam excites only the fluorescent molecules lying within about one wavelength of the substrate, which isolates the regions where cells touch it.

## Why this source

It shows how a piece of textbook optics (total internal reflection and its evanescent field) became a cell biology method still used to image events at the plasma membrane with little background from the rest of the cell.

## Coverage

Citation: Axelrod D. "Cell-substrate contacts illuminated by total internal reflection fluorescence". *Journal of Cell Biology* 89(1):141-145 (1981).
