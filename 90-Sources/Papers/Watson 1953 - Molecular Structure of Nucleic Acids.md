# Watson 1953 - Molecular Structure of Nucleic Acids

> [!abstract]
> The one-page letter that proposed the double-helix structure of DNA, with complementary base pairing inside the helix.

## Why this source

Published on 25 April 1953, "Molecular Structure of Nucleic Acids: A Structure for Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid" gave biology its central structure and suggested a "possible copying mechanism for the genetic material", which opened the path to the genetic code and, 50 years later, the human genome sequence. Watson and Crick shared the 1962 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Maurice Wilkins.

## How to use it

- L1: read it in full (about one page, one figure) when studying [[DNA]]; it is short and readable.
- L2: read with the companion X-ray papers of Wilkins et al. and Franklin and Gosling in the same issue, and with [[Meselson 1958 - The Replication of DNA in Escherichia coli]] which tested the copying hypothesis.

## Caveats

- A model paper: the experimental X-ray evidence came from Rosalind Franklin's and Maurice Wilkins's groups.
- Open-access status of the publisher PDF not verified; copies circulate on university and archive sites.
