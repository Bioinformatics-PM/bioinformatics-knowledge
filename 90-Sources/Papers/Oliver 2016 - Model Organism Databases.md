# Oliver 2016 - Model Organism Databases

> [!abstract]
> A short argument that professionally curated databases are essential to biomedical research and that cutting their funding would be scientifically and economically unsound.

## Why this source

Bioinformaticians depend on curated databases every day and rarely ask who pays for them. This paper makes the case that their sustainability is under threat from changes in funders' policies, presents data suggesting that cuts would be economically unsound, and discusses individual databases such as SGD, PomBase and TAIR.
