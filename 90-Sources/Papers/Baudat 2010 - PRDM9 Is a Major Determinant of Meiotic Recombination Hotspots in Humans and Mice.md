# Baudat 2010 - PRDM9 Is a Major Determinant of Meiotic Recombination Hotspots in Humans and Mice

> [!abstract]
> The paper that identified the zinc-finger protein PRDM9 as a major determinant of where meiotic crossovers concentrate (recombination hotspots) in mice and humans.

## Why this source

Recombination is not spread evenly along chromosomes, and this paper explains much of where it happens: a DNA-binding protein whose binding specificity differs between alleles. It is the primary source for the idea that recombination maps depend on the population and on individual genotypes.

## Coverage

Citation: Baudat F, Buard J, Grey C, Fledel-Alon A, Ober C, Przeworski M, Coop G, de Massy B. "PRDM9 is a major determinant of meiotic recombination hotspots in humans and mice". *Science* 327(5967):836-840 (2010). doi:10.1126/science.1183439. PMID 20044539.

## Caveats

- Hotspot biology has moved fast since 2010 (other species, PRDM9-independent hotspots); treat the paper as the starting point, not the full picture.
