# Pautasso 2013 - Ten Simple Rules for Writing a Literature Review

> [!abstract]
> Ten practical rules for writing a review of the literature, from defining the topic and searching to choosing the type of review and staying objective about one's own work.

## Why this source

Written for scientists in any field and free to read, it explains why reviews exist (no one can read every new paper) and, from the writer's side, where their biases come from: useful both to write a review and to read one critically.

## How to use it

- **L1**: read it before using a review to enter a field; rule 9 tells you what to watch for.
- **L3**: follow all ten rules when writing a review or the introduction of a thesis.

## Caveats

- Rules from experience, not an empirical study of review quality.
- Verified in this pass: citation, DOI, PMC record, the rationale of the introduction, rules 4 and 9. Other rules not checked in detail.
