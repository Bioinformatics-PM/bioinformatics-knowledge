# Schnitzer 1997 - Kinesin Hydrolyses One ATP per 8-nm Step

> [!abstract]
> By tracking single kinesin molecules with interferometry, this paper showed that, near zero load, kinesin hydrolyzes one ATP molecule per 8-nm step along a microtubule.

## Why this source

It fixes the coupling between chemistry and mechanics of a model motor: one fuel molecule, one 8-nm step. That ratio turns a stall force into a work per ATP and an efficiency.

## How to use it

- L1 to L2: cite it for the 8-nm step and the one-ATP-per-step coupling.
- L3: read it as an example of extracting a mechanism from fluctuations, not only from averages.

## Caveats

- Paywalled. Only the abstract was verified.
- The coupling was measured near zero load; compare with [[Svoboda 1994 - Force and Velocity Measured for Single Kinesin Molecules]] for behavior under load.
