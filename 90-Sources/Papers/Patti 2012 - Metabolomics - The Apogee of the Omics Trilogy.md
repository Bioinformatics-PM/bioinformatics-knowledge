# Patti 2012 - Metabolomics - The Apogee of the Omics Trilogy

> [!abstract]
> A review arguing that metabolites give the most direct functional readout of cellular biochemistry, and showing how mass spectrometry made untargeted, systems-level metabolomics possible.

## Why this source

It places metabolomics at the end of the genome, transcriptome, proteome chain and explains why measuring small molecules says the most about what a cell is actually doing. It is the reference for the metabolome layer of [[Omics]] until the [[Metabolomics]] note is written.
