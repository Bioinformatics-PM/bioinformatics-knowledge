# Wikus 2022 - Commercial Gigahertz-Class NMR Magnets

> [!abstract]
> A review, by the engineers who built them, of the first commercial NMR magnets above 1 GHz: superconducting magnets that combine low- and high-temperature superconductors.

## Why this source

A peer-reviewed account of the strongest magnets used for biomolecular NMR, with their field strengths and the engineering requirements (homogeneity, drift, quench protection) that distinguish an NMR magnet from a merely strong one.

## Coverage

Citation: Wikus P, Frantz W, Kümmerle R, Vonlanthen P. *Superconductor Science and Technology* 35(3):033001 (2022). Authors from Bruker.
