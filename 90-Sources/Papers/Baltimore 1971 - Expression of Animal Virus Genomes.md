# Baltimore 1971 - Expression of Animal Virus Genomes

> [!abstract]
> The short paper that proposed classifying viruses by how their genomes are transmitted and expressed, the origin of the Baltimore classification.

## Why this source

The primary source of the scheme that still organizes virology teaching: viruses are grouped not by host or shape but by the relation between their genome and their mRNA. Reading it shows how the classes followed from the then recent discoveries of RNA genomes copied by viral enzymes and of reverse transcription.

## Caveats

- A 1971 paper on animal viruses: it predates the discovery of the replication strategy of hepatitis B virus (group VII).
