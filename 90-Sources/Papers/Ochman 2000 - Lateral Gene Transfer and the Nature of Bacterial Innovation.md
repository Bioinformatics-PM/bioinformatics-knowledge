# Ochman 2000 - Lateral Gene Transfer and the Nature of Bacterial Innovation

> [!abstract]
> A review arguing that bacteria, unlike eukaryotes, obtain much of their diversity by acquiring DNA from distantly related organisms, and that these transfers have reshaped the ecology and pathogenicity of bacterial species.

## Why this source

A short, widely cited synthesis that places horizontal gene transfer at the center of bacterial evolution, by contrast with the gradual modification of existing genes that dominates eukaryotic evolution. Good framing for why bacterial genomes are mosaics and why gene trees of bacteria can disagree.
