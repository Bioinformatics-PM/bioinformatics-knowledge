# European Union 2016 - General Data Protection Regulation

> [!abstract]
> The EU regulation on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data, which treats genetic and health data as special categories.

## Why this source

The primary legal text behind every European rule on handling human genomic data. It defines personal, genetic and health data, sets the legal bases for processing, restricts special categories of data, gives specific provisions for scientific research, and governs transfers outside the EU. Any bioinformatician who touches human sequence data in Europe works under it, whether in research or in the clinic.

## Coverage

Citation: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC (General Data Protection Regulation). *Official Journal of the European Union* L 119, 4.5.2016, p. 1-88. CELEX 32016R0679. Applicable since 25 May 2018.

- L3: read the recitals on scientific research and on anonymous data, then Articles 4, 6, 9 and 89; answer item 12 of [[Regulation and Standards]] for a sequencing study you design.
- Compare with [[Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act]] to see how the US handles the same questions differently.

## Caveats

- A legal text: national laws add rules for health and research data, and guidance from data protection authorities matters in practice.
- Use the consolidated version on EUR-Lex, which includes any corrigenda.
