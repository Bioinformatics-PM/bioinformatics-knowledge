# Green 2016 - Precipitation of DNA with Ethanol

> [!abstract]
> "Precipitation of DNA with Ethanol": a bench protocol by Green and Sambrook for recovering and concentrating DNA from an aqueous solution by precipitating it with ethanol and collecting it by centrifugation.

## Why this source

Ethanol precipitation is the routine way to concentrate DNA and change its buffer. This is the protocol as published by the authors of the 4th edition of *Molecular Cloning: A Laboratory Manual*, in Cold Spring Harbor Protocols, a peer-reviewed methods journal.

## Coverage

Citation: Green MR, Sambrook J. "Precipitation of DNA with Ethanol". *Cold Spring Harbor Protocols* (2016). doi:10.1101/pdb.prot093377.
