# Jacob 1961 - Genetic Regulatory Mechanisms in the Synthesis of Proteins

> [!abstract]
> The long paper in which François Jacob and Jacques Monod proposed the operon model: regulator genes make repressors that act on operators to switch coordinately controlled structural genes, with small molecules controlling the repressor; it also argued for a short-lived messenger RNA.

## Why this source

The founding text of gene regulation. It introduced the vocabulary still in use (regulator gene, repressor, operator, operon, inducer) and the idea that the genome contains control elements as well as protein-coding genes. Jacob and Monod shared the 1965 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with André Lwoff.
