# IMDRF 2013 - Software as a Medical Device Key Definitions

> [!abstract]
> The international regulators' document that defines Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), the concept used to decide whether analysis software is itself a regulated device.

## Why this source

Software that interprets patient data can be a medical device on its own, without any hardware. The International Medical Device Regulators Forum, a group of regulators including the FDA and the European Commission, agreed on a common definition and related terms so that expectations for such software would converge across jurisdictions. It is the starting point for asking whether a bioinformatics tool with a clinical purpose is a regulated product.

## Caveats

- Not law: each jurisdiction applies the idea through its own regulation (the MDR and IVDR in the EU, FDA rules in the US).
- IMDRF documents are revised over time; check the IMDRF site for later software guidance.
