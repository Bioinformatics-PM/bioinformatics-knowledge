# Nurk 2022 - The Complete Sequence of a Human Genome

> [!abstract]
> The Telomere-to-Telomere Consortium's gapless 3.055-billion-base-pair human genome sequence, T2T-CHM13, covering every chromosome except Y.

## Why this source

Two decades after the draft, about 8% of the human genome was still unfinished. Using long-read sequencing, the T2T Consortium completed it: centromeric satellite arrays, recent segmental duplications and the short arms of all five acrocentric chromosomes. The assembly corrected errors in prior references and added nearly 200 million base pairs, containing 1,956 gene predictions of which 99 are predicted to be protein coding.

## Caveats

- One genome from a cell line (CHM13) that is essentially homozygous; it is not a representative of human diversity, which pangenome references address.
- The Y chromosome was completed later, in a separate study.
