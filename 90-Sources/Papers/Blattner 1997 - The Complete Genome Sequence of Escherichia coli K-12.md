# Blattner 1997 - The Complete Genome Sequence of Escherichia coli K-12

> [!abstract]
> The complete 4,639,221-base-pair genome sequence of *Escherichia coli* K-12, the best-studied model bacterium.

## Why this source

The genome of biology's main model organism, and a milestone of early genomics. The annotation of 4,288 protein-coding genes found that 38% had no attributed function, a striking measure of how much remained unknown even in the most studied cell. Comparison with other sequenced microbes revealed widely shared and narrowly distributed gene families; the largest family of paralogous proteins contained 80 ABC transporters.
