# Lloyd 2018 - Phylogenetically Novel Uncultured Microbial Cells Dominate Earth Microbiomes

> [!abstract]
> Uses 16S rRNA gene sequences from metagenomes to estimate how much of Earth's microbial life belongs to lineages without any cultured representative.

## Why this source

A quantitative, open-access estimate of the "uncultured majority", the reason most microbes are studied through their DNA sequences rather than in culture.

## Coverage

Citation: Lloyd KG, Steen AD, Ladau J, Yin J, Crosby L. "Phylogenetically novel uncultured microbial cells dominate Earth microbiomes". *mSystems* 3(5):e00055-18 (2018).

## How to use it

- L2: read the abstract and the introduction with [[Microorganism]] and [[Microbial Ecology]].
- L3: study how 16S rRNA gene fragments from metagenomes are compared with sequences of cultured organisms, an application of [[16S Ribosomal RNA]] and [[Taxonomic Classification]].

## Caveats

- Estimates depend on the databases of cultured organisms at the time and on extrapolation from sampled environments; treat the percentages as orders of magnitude.
- Metadata and the headline estimates verified in this pass (via the PubMed Central record).
