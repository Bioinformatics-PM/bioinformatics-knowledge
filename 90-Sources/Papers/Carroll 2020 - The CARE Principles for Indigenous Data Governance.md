# Carroll 2020 - The CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance

> [!abstract]
> The formal description of the CARE principles (Collective benefit, Authority to control, Responsibility, Ethics), which complement FAIR with people- and purpose-oriented rules for Indigenous data.

## Why this source

FAIR asks whether data can be found and reused; CARE asks who benefits and who decides. The principles were developed by the International Indigenous Data Sovereignty Interest Group of the Research Data Alliance, in consultation with Indigenous Peoples, scholars, non-profit organizations and governments. For genomics, where samples from Indigenous communities have been used without their control, it defines what responsible reuse means.
