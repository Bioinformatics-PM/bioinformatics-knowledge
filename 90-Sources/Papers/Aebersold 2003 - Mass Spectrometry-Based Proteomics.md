# Aebersold 2003 - Mass Spectrometry-Based Proteomics

> [!abstract]
> A landmark review of how mass spectrometry identifies and quantifies proteins: ionization, mass analysers, tandem MS and the proteomics strategies built on them.

## Why this source

The standard entry point to proteomics by mass spectrometry, cited across the field. It defines the parts of a mass spectrometer and compares the mass analysers used for proteins and peptides, including the trapping instruments that measure ion frequencies in a magnetic field.
