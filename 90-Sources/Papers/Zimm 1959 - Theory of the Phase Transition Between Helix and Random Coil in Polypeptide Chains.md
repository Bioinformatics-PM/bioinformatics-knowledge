# Zimm 1959 - Theory of the Phase Transition Between Helix and Random Coil in Polypeptide Chains

> [!abstract]
> The founding statistical-mechanical model of the helix-coil transition: a chain of units that are helical or coil, with one weight for continuing a helix and a small correction factor for starting one.

## Why this source

The original paper of the Zimm-Bragg model, the reference for the nucleation (σ) and propagation (s) parameters that every later treatment of helix formation and of cooperative chain transitions uses.

## Coverage

Citation: Zimm BH, Bragg JK. "Theory of the Phase Transition between Helix and Random Coil in Polypeptide Chains". *The Journal of Chemical Physics* 31(2):526 (1959). doi:10.1063/1.1730390.
