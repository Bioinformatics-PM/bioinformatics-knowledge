# Saiki 1988 - Primer-Directed Enzymatic Amplification of DNA

> [!abstract]
> "Primer-directed enzymatic amplification of DNA with a thermostable DNA polymerase": the paper that turned PCR into the modern method by using Taq polymerase.

## Why this source

Earlier PCR used the Klenow fragment, which is destroyed at denaturation temperature and had to be added again every cycle. Using the heat-stable polymerase from *Thermus aquaticus* made PCR simple, automatable and more specific. It is the most cited of the early PCR papers.

## Caveats

- Not the first PCR paper: PCR was first described with the Klenow fragment (Saiki et al., *Science*, 1985). Kary Mullis, a co-author here, received the 1993 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for inventing PCR.
