# Sanger 1977 - DNA Sequencing with Chain-Terminating Inhibitors

> [!abstract]
> The dideoxy chain-termination method of DNA sequencing, which dominated sequencing for three decades.

## Why this source

This method produced the first genomes and, in automated form, the Human Genome Project. Sanger shared half of the 1980 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Walter Gilbert for determining base sequences in nucleic acids. It is the conceptual ancestor of sequencing-by-synthesis.
