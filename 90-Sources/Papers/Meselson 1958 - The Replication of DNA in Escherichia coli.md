# Meselson 1958 - The Replication of DNA in Escherichia coli

> [!abstract]
> The density-labeling experiment showing that DNA replication is semiconservative: each daughter molecule keeps one parental strand.

## Why this source

Often called "the most beautiful experiment in biology", it tested the copying mechanism suggested by [[Watson 1953 - Molecular Structure of Nucleic Acids|Watson and Crick]] against conservative and dispersive alternatives with a clean, quantitative design.

## Coverage

Citation: Meselson M, Stahl FW. *PNAS* 44(7):671-682 (1958). doi:10.1073/pnas.44.7.671. Free at PMC528642.

## How to use it

- L1: read the figures when writing [[DNA Replication]]; predict the bands for each model before looking at the result.
- L2: use it as a model of hypothesis testing by design (three models, one decisive experiment).

## Caveats

- Shows semiconservative segregation of strands, not the enzymology of replication.
