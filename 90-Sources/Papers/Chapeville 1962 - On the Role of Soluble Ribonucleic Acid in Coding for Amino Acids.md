# Chapeville 1962 - On the Role of Soluble Ribonucleic Acid in Coding for Amino Acids

> [!abstract]
> The experiment that tested the adaptor hypothesis directly: an amino acid chemically changed while attached to its tRNA is inserted where the tRNA, not the amino acid, dictates.

## Why this source

The authors charged tRNA^Cys (then called soluble RNA, sRNA) with cysteine, then converted the attached cysteine into alanine with Raney nickel, without breaking its bond to the tRNA. In a cell-free system programmed with poly(UG), a template that normally directs cysteine incorporation, the hybrid Ala-tRNA^Cys inserted alanine. The position of an amino acid is therefore set by base pairing between the template and the tRNA carrying it, as Crick's adaptor hypothesis predicted. It is the landmark experiment behind [[Transfer RNA]].
