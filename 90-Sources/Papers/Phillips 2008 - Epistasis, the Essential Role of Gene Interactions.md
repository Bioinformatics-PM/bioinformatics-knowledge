# Phillips 2008 - Epistasis, the Essential Role of Gene Interactions

> [!abstract]
> A review of epistasis that separates its meanings (functional interaction of gene products, the masking of one mutation by another in a pathway, and statistical departure from additivity) and argues for studying gene interactions in one quantitative framework.

## Why this source

The word epistasis means different things to a molecular geneticist, a classical geneticist and a quantitative geneticist, and confusion between them is common. This review is the standard reference that names and relates the meanings, from Bateson's masking to Fisher's statistical sense, and connects them to pathways, systems biology and evolution.
