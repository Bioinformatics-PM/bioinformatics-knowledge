# Student 1908 - The Probable Error of a Mean

> [!abstract]
> "The Probable Error of a Mean": the paper in which W. S. Gosset, writing as "Student", derived the distribution of the mean of a small normal sample divided by its estimated standard deviation, the origin of Student's t distribution.

## Why this source

The landmark for small-sample inference. Before it, standard errors estimated from a few measurements were treated as if they were exact; Gosset worked out the sampling distribution that accounts for estimating the standard deviation from the same small sample. Every t-interval and t-test computed on three replicates descends from it.

## Caveats

- Uses the notation and terminology of 1908 (for example "probable error"), which differ from modern textbooks.
- The author's identity behind the pseudonym is established by historical accounts, not stated in the paper.
