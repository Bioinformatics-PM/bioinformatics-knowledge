# Kimsey 2015 - Visualizing Transient Watson-Crick-Like Mispairs in DNA and RNA Duplexes

> [!abstract]
> NMR relaxation-dispersion measurements showing that G·T and G·U wobble mispairs briefly adopt a Watson-Crick-like geometry through rare enol tautomers or anionic bases.

## Why this source

The rare-tautomer hypothesis of spontaneous mutation dates from [[Watson 1953 - Genetical Implications of the Structure of Deoxyribonucleic Acid]], and this paper detected the corresponding short-lived, low-populated states in DNA and RNA duplexes and estimated how often such mispairs form, which links a 1953 hypothesis to measured probabilities.

## Coverage

Citation: Kimsey IJ, Petzold K, Sathyamoorthy B, Stein ZW, Al-Hashimi HM. "Visualizing transient Watson-Crick-like mispairs in DNA and RNA duplexes". *Nature* 519(7543):315-320 (2015). doi:10.1038/nature14227
