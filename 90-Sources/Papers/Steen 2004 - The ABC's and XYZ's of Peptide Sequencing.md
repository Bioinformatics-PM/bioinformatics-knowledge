# Steen 2004 - The ABC's and XYZ's of Peptide Sequencing

> [!abstract]
> A tutorial review of how mass spectrometry identifies and sequences peptides: digestion of proteins, liquid chromatography, mass measurement, fragmentation in tandem MS and interpretation of the spectra.

## Why this source

Written for molecular and cell biologists rather than mass spectrometrists, it is a classic entry point to the vocabulary of peptide mass spectrometry (monoisotopic mass, isotope distributions, fragment ions), widely assigned in bioinformatics courses.

## Coverage

Citation: Steen H, Mann M. *Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology* 5:699-711 (2004).

## How to use it

- L2: read before computing peptide masses or reading a first spectrum.
- L3: return to it for fragment ion nomenclature when writing a theoretical spectrum generator ([[Theoretical Spectrum]]).

## Caveats

- 2004: instruments and acquisition strategies have moved on (e.g. [[Data-Independent Acquisition]]); the physical and chemical principles have not.
- Bibliographic data and the workflow summary were verified; the mass-measurement row (monoisotopic mass, isotope distributions) was confirmed only indirectly, through course material built on this review, not by reading the section. Cite it at article level.
- Access status (paywall) not verified; the abstract is free on the publisher's page.
