# Yakovchuk 2006 - Base-Stacking and Base-Pairing Contributions into Thermal Stability of the DNA Double Helix

> [!abstract]
> Measurements that separate the two sources of double-helix stability, stacking between neighboring bases and pairing between complementary bases.

## Why this source

Textbooks often attribute DNA stability to hydrogen bonds between base pairs. By studying DNA molecules with single nicks and gaps, the authors measured the temperature and salt dependence of the stacking free energy directly, for the first time without extrapolation over the range from below room temperature to near melting. The result is the reference for how much stacking, rather than pairing, holds the helix together.
