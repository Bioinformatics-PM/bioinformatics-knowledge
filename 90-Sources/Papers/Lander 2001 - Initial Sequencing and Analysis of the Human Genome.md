# Lander 2001 - Initial Sequencing and Analysis of the Human Genome

> [!abstract]
> The public Human Genome Project's report of a draft human genome sequence and its first genome-wide analysis.

## Why this source

The landmark of genomics: it presented the draft sequence and analyzed gene content, repeats (for example the genome-wide distribution of young and old Alu copies), GC content and comparisons with other genomes. It launched the data-driven era in which bioinformatics became central to biology.

- L2: read the introduction and the "key findings" sections for context in [[Genome]] and [[Genomics|Human Genome Project]] notes.
- L3: use individual sections (repeats, GC content) as case studies for genome analysis projects.

## Caveats

- A draft: the gene count estimate was later revised downward, and the "complete" telomere-to-telomere sequence came only in 2022.
- The author list is several hundred names; cite as the consortium. The competing Celera paper (Venter et al., *Science*, 2001) is a separate source.
