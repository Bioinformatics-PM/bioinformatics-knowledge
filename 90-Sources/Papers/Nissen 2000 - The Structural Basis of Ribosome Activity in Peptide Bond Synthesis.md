# Nissen 2000 - The Structural Basis of Ribosome Activity in Peptide Bond Synthesis

> [!abstract]
> The atomic structure of the large ribosomal subunit bound to substrate analogues, showing that only RNA surrounds the site where peptide bonds form: the ribosome is a ribozyme.

## Why this source

Using atomic structures of the large ribosomal subunit of the archaeon *Haloarcula marismortui* in complex with two substrate analogues, the authors found that both analogues are contacted exclusively by conserved nucleotides of domain V of the 23S rRNA, and that no protein side-chain atom lies closer than about 18 Å to the peptide bond being made. This settled that the peptidyl transferase activity of the [[Ribosome]] is catalysed by RNA.

## Caveats

- The detailed chemical mechanism proposed in the paper was a hypothesis; read later reviews before quoting it. The finding that the active site is made of RNA is the durable result.
- Steitz shared the 2009 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Venkatraman Ramakrishnan and Ada Yonath for studies of the structure and function of the ribosome.
