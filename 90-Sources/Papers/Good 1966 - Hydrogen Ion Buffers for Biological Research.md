# Good 1966 - Hydrogen Ion Buffers for Biological Research

> [!abstract]
> The paper that set out what a buffer for biological research should be and introduced a family of zwitterionic buffers, HEPES among them, now called "Good's buffers".

## Why this source

Before this work, few buffers with a pKa between 6 and 8 were available to biologists. Good and colleagues listed the properties a biological buffer needs and synthesized and tested new compounds that meet them. It is the primary reference for why laboratory media and enzyme assays use buffers such as HEPES, and for the checklist used to choose one.

## Coverage

Citation: Good NE, Winget GD, Winter W, Connolly TN, Izawa S, Singh RMM. "Hydrogen ion buffers for biological research". *Biochemistry* 5(2):467-477 (1966). PubMed 5942950.

## How to use it

- L1: the list of criteria is the checklist for choosing a buffer; read it with [[Buffer Solution]].
- L2: compare the criteria with the properties of older buffers (phosphate, Tris) to see why no single buffer suits every experiment.

## Caveats

- Verified in this pass: title, authors, journal, volume, issue, pages, year and PubMed ID. The list of criteria and the HEPES pKa are summarized from secondary descriptions of the paper; the full text is paywalled and was not read.
- pKa values of these buffers depend on temperature and ionic strength; use values measured at the working conditions.
