# Shendure 2008 - Next-Generation DNA Sequencing

> [!abstract]
> A review of the massively parallel sequencing platforms that replaced Sanger sequencing for most applications, and of the challenges they created.

## Why this source

Written when the new platforms had just become widely available, it explains their common principle: many DNA fragments immobilized and sequenced in parallel by cycles of enzymatic extension and imaging. It notes that these platforms had cut sequencing costs by more than two orders of magnitude in three years, and names the challenges that followed: library preparation protocols, data analysis and experimental design. It connects replication chemistry (polymerase, primer, 5' to 3' synthesis) to the technology.
