# Li 2014 - System Wide Analyses Have Underestimated Protein Abundances

> [!abstract]
> "System wide analyses have underestimated protein abundances and the importance of transcription in mammals": a reanalysis showing that measurement error hides how much of protein variation mRNA levels explain.

## Why this source

It reanalyses the data of [[Schwanhäusser 2011 - Global Quantification of Mammalian Gene Expression Control]] and argues that errors in the protein and mRNA measurements pull the observed correlation down. For the 4,212 genes they analysed, mRNA levels explain at least 56 % of the differences in protein abundance, and a second, independent strategy suggests about 81 %. The lesson for data analysis is general: a low correlation between two noisy measurements is a lower bound on the true relationship.
