# Mangs 2007 - The Human Pseudoautosomal Region

> [!abstract]
> A review of the two pseudoautosomal regions (PAR1 and PAR2) of the human X and Y chromosomes: where they lie, why they pair and recombine in male meiosis, and the genes they carry.

## Why this source

The X and Y chromosomes are not entirely different: their tips are homologous. This short, free review explains the exception that every analysis of sex chromosomes must handle, from inheritance patterns to variant calling on chrX and chrY.

## Coverage

Citation: Mangs and Morris. "The Human Pseudoautosomal Region (PAR): Origin, Function and Future". *Current Genomics* 8(2):129-136 (2007). Free at PMC2435358.
