# Hasin 2017 - Multi-Omics Approaches to Disease

> [!abstract]
> An open-access review of the high-throughput omics technologies (genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics and others) and of the strategies for integrating several omics layers to understand disease.

## Why this source

It surveys the omics layers side by side, with the kind of data each produces, and then asks the question that motivates [[Multi-Omics Integration]]: how to combine layers to follow the flow of information from genotype to disease. Short, recent enough and free, it is the best single overview for the [[Omics]] note.
