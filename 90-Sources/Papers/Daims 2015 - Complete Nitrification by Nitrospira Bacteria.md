# Daims 2015 - Complete Nitrification by Nitrospira Bacteria

> [!abstract]
> The discovery of comammox: a *Nitrospira* bacterium that oxidizes ammonia all the way to nitrate on its own, identified by cultivation and by finding both ammonia- and nitrite-oxidation pathways in its genome.

## Why this source

Nitrification had long been described as a division of labor between ammonia oxidizers and nitrite oxidizers. This paper shows that a single organism can do both, and the decisive evidence came from genome analysis of an enrichment culture. It is a clear case of a metabolism found through sequence, and of why the metabolic label attached to a lineage in a database is a hypothesis, not a fact.
