# Crick 1970 - Central Dogma of Molecular Biology

> [!abstract]
> Crick's restatement of the central dogma (first proposed in 1957): sequence information cannot be transferred from protein to protein or to nucleic acid.

## Why this source

Written after Temin and Baltimore discovered that some RNA viruses direct DNA synthesis (reverse transcription), it clarifies what the dogma does and does not claim. It is the precise source for a concept that is often misquoted as "DNA makes RNA makes protein".
