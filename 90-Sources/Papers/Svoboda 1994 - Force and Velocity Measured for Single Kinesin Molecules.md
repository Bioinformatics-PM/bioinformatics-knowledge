# Svoboda 1994 - Force and Velocity Measured for Single Kinesin Molecules

> [!abstract]
> Single kinesin molecules attached to beads were loaded with calibrated piconewton forces in an optical trap: their velocity fell linearly with load, and they moved against loads up to 5-6 pN.

## Why this source

The classic single-molecule measurement of a motor's force-velocity curve and stall force, the numbers behind every estimate of the work a motor does per step.

## Coverage

Citation: Svoboda K, Block SM. *Cell* 77(5):773-784 (1994). PubMed 8205624.

## How to use it

- L2: cite it for the stall force of kinesin (5-6 pN).
- L3: read with [[Schnitzer 1997 - Kinesin Hydrolyses One ATP per 8-nm Step]], which found tight coupling of one ATP per step at low load.

## Caveats

- Paywalled. Only the abstract (method, linear force-velocity relation, 5-6 pN loads, loose-coupling suggestion) was verified.
