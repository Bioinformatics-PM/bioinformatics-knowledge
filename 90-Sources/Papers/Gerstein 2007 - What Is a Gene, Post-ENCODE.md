# Gerstein 2007 - What Is a Gene, Post-ENCODE

> [!abstract]
> A history of the gene concept and a proposed definition for the genomic era: a gene is a union of genomic sequences encoding a coherent set of potentially overlapping functional products.

## Why this source

The ENCODE pilot project revealed dispersed regulation, pervasive transcription and many noncoding RNAs, which strained every classical definition of a gene. This review traces the concept from Mendel's factors through "one gene, one enzyme" to annotated open reading frames, then proposes a definition centred on final functional products (proteins or RNAs) and deliberately leaves regulatory regions out. It is the best single text for the question "what is a gene?".
