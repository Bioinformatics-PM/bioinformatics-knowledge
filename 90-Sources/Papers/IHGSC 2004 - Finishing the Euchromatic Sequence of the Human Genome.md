# IHGSC 2004 - Finishing the Euchromatic Sequence of the Human Genome

> [!abstract]
> The Human Genome Project's report of the finished euchromatic sequence (Build 35), which also lowered the protein-coding gene estimate to 20,000 to 25,000.

## Why this source

It closed the public project: 2.85 billion nucleotides interrupted by only 341 gaps, covering about 99% of the euchromatic genome, with an error rate of about 1 event per 100,000 bases. With a finished sequence, the consortium revised the gene count down from the 30,000 to 40,000 of the 2001 draft to 20,000 to 25,000 protein-coding genes, a lesson in how estimates depend on data quality and definitions.

## Caveats

- "Finished" meant the euchromatin only: heterochromatin, centromeres and many repeats remained missing until 2022.
