# Woese 1977 - Phylogenetic Structure of the Prokaryotic Domain

> [!abstract]
> "Phylogenetic structure of the prokaryotic domain: the primary kingdoms": ribosomal RNA sequences reveal three aboriginal lines of descent, the discovery of the archaea.

## Why this source

Woese and Fox characterized fragments of ribosomal RNA sequences (oligonucleotide catalogues of the small-subunit rRNA) from many organisms and used them to infer relationships. Living systems fell into three primary lines of descent: the eubacteria (typical bacteria), the archaebacteria (at the time, the methanogens) and the "urkaryotes", represented by the cytoplasmic component of eukaryotic cells. It made rRNA the universal molecular yardstick of phylogeny, the idea behind [[16S Ribosomal RNA]] analyses today.

## Caveats

- A 1977 method (oligonucleotide catalogues, not full sequences); the terms "archaebacteria" and "urkaryotes" were later replaced. Read it for the method and the tripartite result, and a current text for the present view of the tree.
