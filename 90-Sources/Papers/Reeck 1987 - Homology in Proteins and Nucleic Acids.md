# Reeck 1987 - Homology in Proteins and Nucleic Acids

> [!abstract]
> A short invited article by leading molecular evolutionists asking that "homology" be used only in its precise biological sense, common evolutionary origin, and never as a synonym of similarity.

## Why this source

It is the classic reference for a distinction every sequence analyst must make. "Homology" means having a common evolutionary origin, but it was widely used in the loose sense of "similarity", which the authors say clogs the sequence-comparison literature with muddy writing and sometimes muddy thinking. Sequences are either homologous or not; they cannot have a "level" or a "percent" of homology, whereas they do have a measurable level of similarity.
