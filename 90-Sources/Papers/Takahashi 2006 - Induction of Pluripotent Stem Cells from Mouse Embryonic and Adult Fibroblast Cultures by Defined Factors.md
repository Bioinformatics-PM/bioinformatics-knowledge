# Takahashi 2006 - Induction of Pluripotent Stem Cells from Mouse Embryonic and Adult Fibroblast Cultures by Defined Factors

> [!abstract]
> The paper that created induced pluripotent stem (iPS) cells: four transcription factors turned mouse fibroblasts into cells that behave like embryonic stem cells.

## Why this source

It showed that a differentiated cell's identity can be reset by a handful of transcription factors, without an egg or an embryo. That result, recognized by the 2012 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine ([[NobelPrize.org]]), is the experimental proof that differentiation is maintained by regulatory networks, and the starting point of patient-derived stem cell models.

## Coverage

Citation: Takahashi K, Yamanaka S. "Induction of pluripotent stem cells from mouse embryonic and adult fibroblast cultures by defined factors". *Cell* 126(4):663-676 (2006). PMID 16904174.

## How to use it

- **L2**: read the abstract with [[Stem Cell]] and [[Cell Differentiation]]: what was added, what was obtained, how pluripotency was tested.

## Caveats

- Mouse cells only; human iPS cells came in later papers, not covered here.
- Verified in this pass (web search): the citation and PMID, the four factors, the ES culture conditions, Nanog not being required, the ES-like properties, the teratoma and blastocyst tests. The DOI and the details of the initial candidate screen were not verified.
