# Nelson 2015 - The Support of Human Genetic Evidence for Approved Drug Indications

> [!abstract]
> Evidence that drug mechanisms supported by human genetics are more likely to succeed in development, with an estimated doubling of clinical success rates.

## Why this source

It turned an intuition into a measurable argument: link each drug target and indication to human genetic associations, then count how often genetic support appears at each stage of development. The share of mechanisms with direct genetic support rose from 2.0% at the preclinical stage to 8.2% among approved drugs, and varied a lot between disease areas. This is the paper behind the industry practice of prioritizing genetically supported targets.

## Caveats

- Association with success is not proof that genetics causes success; confounding by target class and disease area is possible, and later analyses with larger datasets have revisited the size of the effect.
