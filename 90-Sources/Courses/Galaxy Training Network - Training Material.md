# Galaxy Training Network - Training Material

> [!abstract]
> Community-maintained collection of hands-on tutorials for data analysis in the Galaxy platform, organized by field and technology, from getting started to end-to-end sequencing analyses.

## Why this source

- Hundreds of step-by-step tutorials (about 525 across 35 topics, by about 527 contributors when checked), each runnable on a public Galaxy server without installing anything.
- Covers real workflows (raw reads to biological interpretation) that lecture courses rarely reach.

## How to use it

- **Level L2 to L3**, once you know what sequencing reads are ([[Next-Generation Sequencing]]).
- Recommended order (from the site): Introduction to Galaxy Analyses, then Sequence analysis, then Transcriptomics.
- Redo one analysis on the command line afterwards ([[MIT - The Missing Semester of Your CS Education]], [[Coursera JHU - Genomic Data Science Specialization]]) to see what Galaxy hides.

## Caveats

- Counts change continuously; tutorial quality and depth vary because authors vary.
- Graphical workflow tool: complements, but does not replace, scripting skills.
