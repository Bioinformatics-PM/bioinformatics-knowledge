# EMBL-EBI - Introductory Bioinformatics

> [!abstract]
> EMBL-EBI's free, self-paced "Introductory bioinformatics pathway": a curated collection of short online courses introducing bioinformatics and touring EMBL-EBI resources such as Ensembl, UniProt and Expression Atlas.

## Why this source

- The gentlest hands-on entry to biological databases, from the institute that runs many of them; aimed at bench researchers with little or no bioinformatics experience.
- Short modules (the interactive "A journey through bioinformatics" takes about an hour), progress tracked with a free account.

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, alongside [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]]: do "Bioinformatics for the terrified" first, then one database module per biology unit, always with the database open in another tab.
- An undergraduate life-science background helps but is not required.
- Next: [[Galaxy Training Network - Training Material]] for analysis workflows.

## Caveats

- No certificates for on-demand courses.
- Database interfaces change faster than tutorials; if a screenshot no longer matches, follow the current interface.
