# MIT 7.016 - Introductory Biology

> [!abstract]
> MIT's introductory biology course (Fall 2018 run) covering biochemistry, molecular biology, genetics and cell biology, with an emphasis on chemical biology, therapeutics and research tools.

## Why this source

- More recent than [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]], with a stronger chemical-biology and human-health angle ("twenty-first-century molecular genetics" and therapeutic intervention).
- Full materials: lecture videos, problem sets with solutions, exams with solutions, recitation handouts.
- MIT's introductory biology variants (7.012 to 7.016) share the same core material, so this run is a valid substitute for any of them.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Biochemistry | Biological molecules, chemical biology | [[Protein]], [[Nucleic Acid]], [[Enzyme]] |
| Molecular biology and genetics | Structure and regulation of genes; structure and synthesis of proteins | [[Gene]], [[Gene Regulation]], [[Transcription]], [[Translation]], [[Molecular Biology]], [[Genetics]] |
| Cell biology | Integration of molecules into cells, and of cells into multicellular organisms | [[Cell]], [[Cell Biology]] |
| Applications | Therapeutics and tools for advancing research | [[Drug Discovery]], [[Molecular Cloning\|Recombinant DNA]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, first year. Choose it or 7.01SC as the main biology course; use the other for extra problems.
- Watch lectures in order, do each problem set before the recitation handout, and use the exams for timed practice.
- Continue with [[MIT 7.03 - Genetics]] and [[MIT 5.07SC - Biological Chemistry I]].

## Caveats

- A classroom recording, not an OCW Scholar course: less scaffolding (no concept quizzes or help sessions) than 7.01SC.
- The lecture-by-lecture list was not verified; the table follows the course description.
