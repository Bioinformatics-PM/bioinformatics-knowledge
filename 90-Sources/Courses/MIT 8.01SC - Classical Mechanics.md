# MIT 8.01SC - Classical Mechanics

> [!abstract]
> MIT's OCW Scholar first physics course (Fall 2016): space, time, mass, force, momentum, torque and angular momentum, solved both with forces and with conservation laws.

## Why this source

- OCW Scholar course, substantially more complete than a standard OCW site, with custom content: lecture videos, an open textbook and problem sets.
- Teaches the two problem-solving routes (forces, conservation laws) that later carry over to energy landscapes and statistical physics.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Motion and forces | Kinematics; force-based approach to motion | [[Kinematics]], [[Newton's Laws of Motion]] |
| Energy | Conservation of energy | [[Conservation of Energy]] |
| Momentum | Conservation of momentum | [[Momentum]] |
| Rotation | Torque and angular momentum | [[Mechanics\|Torque]], [[Mechanics\|Angular Momentum]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, with [[MIT 18.01SC - Single Variable Calculus]] in parallel.
- For bioinformatics, do the energy and momentum parts thoroughly; they are the entry point to [[Statistical Physics|Statistical Mechanics]] and to [[MIT 8.592J - Statistical Physics in Biology]]. Rigid-body rotation can be lighter.

## Caveats

- The course page carries textbook chapters labeled for a 2022 revision alongside the 2016 videos.
- The week-by-week list and the availability of problem-set solutions were not verified; the table follows the course description.
