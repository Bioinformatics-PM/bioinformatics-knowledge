# MIT - The Missing Semester of Your CS Education

> [!abstract]
> Short MIT IAP class on proficiency with computing tools (shell, editors, version control, debugging, packaging), with all materials and lecture recordings free online; this note uses the January 2026 edition.

## Why this source

- Covers what programming courses leave out and every bioinformatics pipeline needs: the command line and git.
- The 2026 edition (9 sessions, 12 to 23 January 2026) integrates AI-based development tools into each lecture and adds a lecture on agentic coding.

## How to use it

- **Level L1 to L2**, as soon as you write your first programs, alongside [[MIT 6.100L - Introduction to CS and Programming Using Python]].
- Do the shell and git lectures first, with their exercises, before [[Galaxy Training Network - Training Material]] or the command-line course of [[Coursera JHU - Genomic Data Science Specialization]].
- The earlier edition, still online, covers shell scripting, vim, tmux, ssh, sed and awk in more depth.

## Caveats

- Student-taught IAP class, not an OCW credit course (tier B).
- The agentic-coding lecture is tool-specific and will date quickly.
