# MIT 18.01SC - Single Variable Calculus

> [!abstract]
> MIT's OCW Scholar calculus course (Fall 2010): differentiation and integration of functions of one variable, ending with infinite series.

## Why this source

- Built for independent study: sessions combine lecture clips (David Jerison, recorded on campus in Fall 2007), notes, problem sets with solutions and exams with solutions.
- Self-contained: the site includes all materials needed to learn the subject.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Differentiation | Limits, derivatives and their applications | [[Limit]], [[Derivative]] |
| Integration | Integrals and techniques of integration | [[Integral]], [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]] |
| Infinite series | Brief treatment of series | [[Infinite Series]], [[Taylor Series]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, first semester. If high-school calculus is solid, move quickly through differentiation and spend the time on integration and series.
- Integration is required for continuous probability ([[Probability Density Function]]) in [[MIT 18.05 - Introduction to Probability and Statistics]], and for [[MIT 18.03SC - Differential Equations]].
- Next: [[MIT 18.02SC - Multivariable Calculus]].
