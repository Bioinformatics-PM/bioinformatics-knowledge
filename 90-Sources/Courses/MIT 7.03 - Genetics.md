# MIT 7.03 - Genetics

> [!abstract]
> MIT's core undergraduate genetics course (Fall 2004): genes, chromosomes and genomes, variation, population genetics and gene regulation, with problem sets and exams with solutions.

## Why this source

- Trains genetic reasoning (crosses, mutants, mapping logic) through many graded problems, which is exactly what later statistical genetics and variant analysis assume.
- Complete assessment set: six problem sets with solutions, three one-hour exams and a final with solutions, plus older problem sets as study material.

## How to use it

- **Level L2**, after [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]] or [[MIT 7.016 - Introductory Biology]].
- Work every problem set on paper before reading the solutions; genetics is learned by solving problems, not by reading notes.
- Later, [[MIT 6.047 - Computational Biology]] (evolution and population genomics part) builds on this.

## Caveats

- 2004 run: lecture notes only, **no lecture videos**. Technology content predates next-generation sequencing; the reasoning content is still current.
- Specific lecture titles were not verified; the table follows the course description.
