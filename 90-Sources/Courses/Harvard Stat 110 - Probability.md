# Harvard Stat 110 - Probability

> [!abstract]
> Joe Blitzstein's Harvard probability course, taught yearly since 2006: a comprehensive introduction to probability as a language and toolset for statistics, science, risk and randomness, with free lectures, problems and a free textbook.

## Why this source

- The best free source for probabilistic problem solving: over 250 practice problems with detailed solutions, and "strategic practice" problems grouped by theme before the mixed homework and exam problems.
- Free lecture videos (YouTube), review material and sample exams; companion textbook *Introduction to Probability* (Blitzstein and Hwang, 2nd ed.) with a free online version; a complementary edX course, Stat110x.

## How to use it

- **Level L2**, after [[MIT 18.05 - Introduction to Probability and Statistics]]; an alternative to [[MIT 6.041SC - Probabilistic Systems Analysis and Applied Probability]] (take one, use the other for problems).
- Per topic: lecture, then strategic practice, then homework, checking solutions only after an attempt.

## Caveats

- `year` is the current version of the course site; the YouTube lectures are older recordings whose year was not verified.
- The limit-theorem row reflects the standard syllabus and the textbook; the playlist summary checked named sample spaces, Bayes' theorem, distributions and Markov chains.
- The course hub was formerly stat110.net.
