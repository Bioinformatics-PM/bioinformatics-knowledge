# HarvardX PH525x - Data Analysis for the Life Sciences

> [!abstract]
> Self-paced HarvardX series (PH525.1x to PH525.7x) by Rafael Irizarry and Michael Love teaching statistical inference and genomics data analysis through R, from p-values to Bioconductor.

## Why this source

- Written by biostatisticians who build the tools used in genomics (Bioconductor); every concept is programmed in R on real life-science data.
- Two series: Data Analysis for Life Sciences (PH525.1x to 4x) and Genomics Data Analysis (PH525.5x to 7x). A companion book by the same authors, *Data Analysis for the Life Sciences*, covers the same material.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| PH525.1x Statistics and R | Statistical inference (p-values, confidence intervals), exploratory data analysis, robust and non-parametric statistics; no R or statistics background assumed | [[Statistical Inference]], [[P-Value]], [[Confidence Interval]], [[Exploratory Data Analysis]], [[Nonparametric Statistics]], [[R Programming\|R (Programming Language)]] |
| PH525.2x to 4x | Linear models and matrix algebra; inference and modeling for high-throughput experiments; high-dimensional data analysis | [[Linear Models\|Linear Model]], [[Multiple Testing Correction\|Multiple Testing]], [[Dimensionality Reduction]] |
| PH525.5x to 7x | Introduction to Bioconductor, case studies in functional genomics, advanced topics including high-performance computing | [[Bioconductor]], [[Transcriptomics\|Functional Genomics]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L2** for PH525.1x (after [[MIT 18.05 - Introduction to Probability and Statistics]]), **L3** for the rest once you analyze omics data.
- Complements [[Coursera JHU - Genomic Data Science Specialization]] (Bioconductor and statistics courses) and [[MIT 18.650 - Statistics for Applications]] (theory).
