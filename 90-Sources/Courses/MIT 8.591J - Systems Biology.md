# MIT 8.591J - Systems Biology

> [!abstract]
> Jeff Gore's MIT systems biology course (Fall 2014), with lecture videos on gene networks and cellular decision-making, evolutionary systems biology and ecological systems biology.

## Why this source

- Quantitative, model-driven view of gene regulation (kinetics, feedback, noise) from MIT's physics department, with lecture videos.
- Bridges differential equations and molecular biology: every lecture turns a biological question into a small dynamical model.

## How to use it

- **Level L3** (the course is open to graduate and undergraduate students), after [[MIT 18.03SC - Differential Equations]] and [[MIT 7.03 - Genetics]].
- Watch lectures in order and re-implement each model in Python (numerical ODE integration, stochastic simulation).

## Caveats

- Only the lecture titles above were verified; whether problem sets with solutions are posted was not checked.
