# Peking University - Bioinformatics Introduction and Methods

> [!abstract]
> Peking University's Coursera course by Ge Gao and Liping Wei (Center for Bioinformatics) introducing the concepts and computational methods of bioinformatics, from alignment and hidden Markov models to next-generation sequencing analysis.

## Why this source

- A compact, method-oriented tour by a leading Chinese bioinformatics center, including the history of the field and bioinformatics in mainland China.
- 14 modules with 13 assignments; hands-on HMM construction and NGS pipeline examination.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |

| Sequence alignment | Needleman-Wunsch global and Smith-Waterman local alignment | [[Sequence Alignment]], [[Needleman-Wunsch Algorithm]], [[Smith-Waterman Algorithm]], [[Dynamic Programming]] |

| Genetic variants | Functional prediction of genetic variants | [[Variant Annotation]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L2**, after [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]] and basic programming.
- Best as a second pass on alignment, HMMs and NGS after [[Coursera UCSD - Bioinformatics Specialization]] courses I to III, or as the main course for Chinese-speaking learners.

## Caveats

- Taught in Chinese (Simplified); only some videos (for example student presentations) carry English subtitles.
- Auditing is free; graded work follows Coursera's paid model. Module titles were not checked individually.
