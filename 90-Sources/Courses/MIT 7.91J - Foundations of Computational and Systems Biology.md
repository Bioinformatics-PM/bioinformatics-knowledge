# MIT 7.91J - Foundations of Computational and Systems Biology

> [!abstract]
> MIT graduate introduction to computational biology (Spring 2014): nucleic acid and protein sequence and structure analysis, plus the analysis of complex biological systems, with full lecture videos.

## Why this source

- Three leading computational biologists, each teaching their area; complementary to [[MIT 6.047 - Computational Biology]] (stronger here on structure and networks).
- Materials: video lectures with transcripts, lecture notes and slides, programming assignments with examples, projects and presentations.

## How to use it

- **Level L3 to M1**. Needs a strong background in either molecular biology or computer science; in this curriculum, take it after [[MIT 6.006 - Introduction to Algorithms]], [[MIT 7.03 - Genetics]] and a probability course.
- Watch the videos in order and do the programming assignments. Use it alongside or after [[MIT 6.047 - Computational Biology]].

## Caveats

- Graduate-level. Grading for graduate students was 30% problem sets, 48% exams, 20% project; whether exam solutions are posted was not verified.
- Lecture-level titles beyond Lecture 1 were not checked; rows follow the course description.
