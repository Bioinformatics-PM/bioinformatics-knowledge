# Khan Academy - Biology

> [!abstract]
> Khan Academy's free Biology library, which combines middle-school, high-school and AP-level biology in short videos, articles and practice exercises.

## Why this source

- Fast, free refreshers on basic biology before or during a university course; each unit has practice questions.
- Goes deeper than Khan Academy's separate High school biology course.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chemistry of life | Units such as "Elements of life" | [[Atom]], [[Biochemistry\|Biological Macromolecule]] |
| Cells | Units such as "More about membranes" | [[Cell]], [[Lipid Bilayer\|Biological Membrane]], [[Cell Biology]] |
| Other units | Genetics, molecular biology and further school-level topics | [[Genetics]], [[Molecular Biology]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, remedial only: watch a short video when a lecture of [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]] assumes background you lack.
- Never the sole support for a claim in a concept note (tier D); cite the MIT or textbook source instead.

## Caveats

- Popularization; school-level depth. Unit structure changes over time and only two unit titles were checked.
