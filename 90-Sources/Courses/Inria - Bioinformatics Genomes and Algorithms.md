# Inria - Bioinformatics Genomes and Algorithms

> [!abstract]
> Inria's five-week MOOC on FUN (in French as "Bioinformatique : algorithmes et génomes", and in English) on how computer science supports the interpretation of genome sequences, with algorithms shown in Python notebooks.

## Why this source

- Gentle, well-paced entry that introduces both the biology and the basic algorithms, by François Rechenmann, research director at Inria for more than 30 years, notably in a computational biology team.
- Algorithms are executable and editable in Python notebooks integrated in the course (Thierry Parmentelat).
- Format: about 10 sequences of 6-minute videos per week, quizzes, course documents and practical exercises at the end of each week.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |

| Phylogenetic trees | Tree reconstruction | [[Phylogenetic Tree]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L1 to L2**, after a first programming course ([[MIT 6.100L - Introduction to CS and Programming Using Python]]); useful for French-speaking learners.
- Run and modify every notebook. Then move to [[Coursera UCSD - Bioinformatics Specialization]] for depth.

## Caveats

- Archived course: contents created in 2015, still open for registration but not facilitated; collaborative spaces are closed and no certificate is issued. Videos are also on Canal-U.
- The mapping of topics to weeks was not checked.
