# Coursera UCSD - Bioinformatics Specialization

> [!abstract]
> UC San Diego's seven-course Coursera series (six courses plus a capstone) teaching how algorithms and software tools answer questions in modern biology, from DNA replication to molecular evolution and mutations.

## Why this source

- Algorithm-first bioinformatics for both biologists and computer scientists; beginner level, no prior experience required.
- The courses mirror core classes of UC San Diego's Bioinformatics and Systems Biology program and cover twice as much material. The first course was named a top-50 MOOC of all time by Class Central.
- Companion textbook by the same authors: *Bioinformatics Algorithms: An Active Learning Approach*.

## Coverage

| Course | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| I. Finding Hidden Messages in DNA | Hidden messages in genomes, starting from DNA replication | [[DNA Replication]], [[Motif Finding]], [[K-mer]] |
| II. Genome Sequencing | Reconstructing genomes from reads | [[Genome Assembly]], [[De Bruijn Graph]] |
| III. Comparing Genes, Proteins, and Genomes | Sequence and genome comparison | [[Sequence Alignment]], [[Dynamic Programming]], [[Genome Rearrangement]] |
| IV. Molecular Evolution | Evolutionary trees | [[Phylogenetic Tree]], [[Molecular Evolution]] |
| V. Genomic Data Science and Clustering | Clustering biological data | [[Clustering]], [[Gene Expression]] |
| VI. Finding Mutations in DNA and Proteins | Detecting variation | [[Mutation]], [[Read Mapping]] |
| Capstone: Big Data in Biology | Project with tools and data from Illumina | [[Next-Generation Sequencing]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L2** for courses I to III, after [[MIT 6.100L - Introduction to CS and Programming Using Python]] and [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]]; **level L3** for IV to VI and the capstone, after [[MIT 6.006 - Introduction to Algorithms]].
- Implement every algorithm in Python yourself before looking at any solution; this is the main hands-on companion to [[MIT 6.047 - Computational Biology]].
- About 3 months at 10 hours per week for the whole series (Coursera estimate).

## Caveats

- Weekly syllabi were not checked; the vault links in the right column follow the course titles and descriptions.
- Course IV is listed both as "Molecular Evolution" and "Deciphering Molecular Evolution".
- Auditing is free; graded work and certificates follow Coursera's paid model.
