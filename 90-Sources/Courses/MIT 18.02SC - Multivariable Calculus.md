# MIT 18.02SC - Multivariable Calculus

> [!abstract]
> MIT's OCW Scholar course (Fall 2010) on differential, integral and vector calculus for functions of more than one variable, led by Denis Auroux.

## Why this source

- OCW Scholar: video lectures, lecture notes, problem sets with solutions, exams with solutions, recitation videos with problem-solving tips, and interactive "Mathlets".
- The standard route to gradients and multiple integrals, needed for optimization and for joint probability densities.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Differential calculus | Functions of several variables, partial derivatives, gradient | [[Partial Derivative]], [[Gradient]] |
| Integral calculus | Multiple integrals | [[Multiple Integral]] |
| Vector calculus | Vector fields, line and surface integrals | [[Calculus\|Vector Field]], [[Calculus\|Line Integral]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L1 (second semester) to L2**, after [[MIT 18.01SC - Single Variable Calculus]].
- Priority for bioinformatics: partial derivatives, gradient and optimization (for [[Maximum Likelihood Estimation]] and [[Gradient Descent]]), and double integrals (joint densities). Flux and Stokes-type theorems can be lighter.

## Caveats

- Unit titles were not checked; rows follow the course description (differential, integral, vector calculus).
- The Mathlets were Java applets; some may not run in current browsers.
