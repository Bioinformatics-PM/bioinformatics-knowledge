# Université Paris-Saclay - Introduction à la statistique avec R

> [!abstract]
> French-language FUN MOOC (about 25 hours, 5 sequences) introducing statistics and the R language, from describing variables to confidence intervals and associations between variables.

## Why this source

- Taught by Bruno Falissard (professor of biostatistics, Université Paris-Saclay) with Christophe Lalanne: introductory statistics from a biostatistics perspective, in French.
- Free, with a free certificate; runs as periodic sessions (latest seen: 3 March to 12 April 2024).

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, a first contact with statistics and R for French-speaking learners, before [[MIT 18.05 - Introduction to Probability and Statistics]].
- Reproduce every analysis in R, then later in Python.
