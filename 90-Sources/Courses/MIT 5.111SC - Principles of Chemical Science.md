# MIT 5.111SC - Principles of Chemical Science

> [!abstract]
> MIT's OCW Scholar general chemistry course (Fall 2014): atomic and molecular electronic structure, thermodynamics, acid-base and redox equilibria, kinetics and catalysis, illustrated with biological, inorganic and organic molecules.

## Why this source

- General chemistry introduced through the chemistry of biological, inorganic and organic molecules, which makes it a good chemistry base for a bioinformatics path.
- OCW Scholar packaging: video lectures, lecture notes and exams, organized by units (Unit I: The Atom).

## How to use it

- **Level L1**, in parallel with [[MIT 7.01SC - Fundamentals of Biology]].
- Priority for bioinformatics: bonding, thermodynamics (free energy), acid-base equilibria and kinetics, which reappear in [[MIT 5.07SC - Biological Chemistry I]] and in [[MIT 8.592J - Statistical Physics in Biology]].

## Caveats

- Only Unit I's title and the course description were verified; the other rows group the description's topics, not official unit names.
- Problem sets were not confirmed in the resource list checked (video lectures, lecture notes and exams were).
