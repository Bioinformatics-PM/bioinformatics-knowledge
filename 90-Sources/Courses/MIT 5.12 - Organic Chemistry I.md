# MIT 5.12 - Organic Chemistry I

> [!abstract]
> MIT's first-semester organic chemistry course (Spring 2005): structure, functional groups, aromaticity and carbonyl chemistry, with problem sets and exams with solutions.

## Why this source

- Complete assessment material: problem sets with solutions, exams and practice exams with answer keys, lecture handouts, readings and calendar.
- The only MIT OCW run of 5.12 found; enough to understand the chemistry of nucleotides, amino acid side chains and cofactors.

## Coverage

From the lecture handout titles.

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Conformational analysis | [[Conformational Analysis]] |
| Functional groups | Alcohols, alkenes, alkynes | [[Functional Group]], [[Functional Group\|Alkene]], [[Functional Group\|Alkyne]] |
| Aromatic chemistry | Aromaticity, electrophilic aromatic substitution, Friedel-Crafts reactions | [[Aromaticity]] |
| Carbonyl chemistry | Carbonyl compounds and their reactions | [[Carbonyl Group]] |

## How to use it

- **Level L2**, after [[MIT 5.111SC - Principles of Chemical Science]], before or alongside [[MIT 5.07SC - Biological Chemistry I]].
- For a bioinformatics path, focus on functional groups, aromaticity (nucleobases, aromatic amino acids) and carbonyl chemistry; skim synthesis-heavy material.
- Do the practice exams with keys as self-tests.

## Caveats

- 2005 run: **no lecture videos**, handouts only. The textbook used by the readings was not verified.
- Handout titles cover only part of the syllabus; the full lecture list was not checked.
