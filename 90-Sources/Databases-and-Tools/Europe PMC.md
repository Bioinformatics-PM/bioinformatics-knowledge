# Europe PMC

> [!abstract]
> An open-access database of life-science journal articles and preprints, searchable over abstracts and, for millions of articles, full text, through a website, APIs and bulk download.

## Why this source

Europe PMC complements [[PubMed]]: besides abstracts it searches full text where available and indexes preprints next to journal articles, which makes it a good second search engine for [[Literature Search]] and a direct route to free full text ([[Open Access]]).

## Coverage

Key publication: "Europe PMC in 2023". *Nucleic Acids Research* 52(D1):D1668 (2024). Free at PMC10767826 (PubMed 37994696). At that time Europe PMC contained over 42 million abstracts and over 9 million full-text articles, accessible via the website, APIs and bulk download.

## How to use it

- **L1**: search a topic in both PubMed and Europe PMC and compare what each finds; open a free full text from Europe PMC.
- **L2**: include preprints in a search, and note which results are not yet peer reviewed ([[Peer Review]]).
- **L3**: use the APIs for full-text searches that PubMed cannot do.
