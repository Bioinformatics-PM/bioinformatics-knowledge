# ELIXIR RDMkit

> [!abstract]
> ELIXIR's research data management toolkit for the life sciences, organizing best practices, tools and national guidance by stage of the data life cycle.

## Why this source

Written by the people who handle life science data every day, it answers practical questions: how to plan, collect, process, share and preserve data in a given domain and country. Launched in March 2021 by the ELIXIR-CONVERGE project, it acts as a hub linking to tool registries, training material, standards, databases and data management plan services. Its organization by life cycle stage, by role, by research domain and by data management problem makes it easy to enter from any question.

## How to use it

- L2: start with the data life cycle pages for items 1 and 2 of [[Research Data Management]], then write a data management plan for a Lab project.
- L3: use the domain and task pages before submitting data (item 14) or handling human data (item 15).

- European focus in the national and legal guidance.
- A living website; pages change, so note the access date when citing a page.
