# IUPAC Gold Book

> [!abstract]
> IUPAC's Compendium of Chemical Terminology online: the reference definitions of chemical terms and quantities, one entry per term with a stable identifier.

## Why this source

When textbooks differ on a definition or a symbol, the IUPAC definition is the one chemistry agrees on. Each entry has a short identifier and its own page, so a note can cite one definition precisely.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entry "ionic strength" (I03180) | $I_m = \frac{1}{2} \sum_B m_B z_B^2$ on a molality basis and $I_c = \frac{1}{2} \sum_B c_B z_B^2$ on a concentration basis, $z_B$ being the charge number of ion B | [[Aqueous Solution]], [[Buffer Solution]] |

## How to use it

- L1-L2: look up a term when a textbook definition is ambiguous; cite the entry by its identifier.

## Caveats

- Only the "ionic strength" entry was checked in this pass (URL pattern `goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/<identifier>`); verify any other entry before citing it.
- Definitions are terse and assume the vocabulary of physical chemistry; pair them with a textbook explanation.
