# NobelPrize.org

> [!abstract]
> The official website of the Nobel Prize, with the press release of each prize; used here for the 2014 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, awarded for super-resolved fluorescence microscopy.

## Why this source

A prize announcement is a dated, authoritative record of which discovery was recognized, for whom and why, written for a general audience. The 2014 chemistry press release states the problem plainly: optical microscopy was long held back by a presumed limit, a resolution never better than half the wavelength of light, and fluorescent molecules made it possible to circumvent it.

## Caveats

- A prize announcement credits a few people for work done by many groups: use it for the landmark and the principle, and primary papers or reviews for method details.
- Verified in this pass, from the 2014 press release: the laureates, the prize motivation, the half-wavelength limit, and the two methods with the date of STED. The longer scientific background documents were not read.
