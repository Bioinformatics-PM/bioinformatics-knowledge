# The Turing Way

> [!abstract]
> An open source, community-driven handbook on reproducible, ethical, inclusive and collaborative data science, hosted by The Alan Turing Institute.

## Why this source

It collects, in one free and openly developed book, the practices that make computational research reproducible and shareable, written by researchers for researchers. Since a 2020 reorganization it is split into five guides (reproducible research, project design, collaboration, communication, ethical research) plus a community handbook, with hundreds of chapters. Releases are archived on Zenodo with a DOI, so it can be cited precisely.

## How to use it

- L2: read the reproducible research guide chapter by chapter while setting up a Lab repository (version control, environment, tests).
- L3: use the project design and ethics guides when planning [[10-genomic-pipeline]] as a research compendium.

## Caveats

- Community-written: depth and quality vary between chapters; it complements rather than replaces primary guidelines such as [[Sandve 2013 - Ten Simple Rules for Reproducible Computational Research]].
- A living book; cite a specific release (Zenodo DOI) when citing it.
