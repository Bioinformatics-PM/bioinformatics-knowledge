# Université Paris Cité - Licence Sciences de la Vie

> [!abstract]
> Université Paris Cité's three-year, 180 ECTS Licence Sciences de la Vie: a common L1 and L2 in life sciences, then four L3 parcours, one of which, Biologie-Informatique (BI), combines life sciences and computer science and leads to bioinformatics masters.

## Why this source

The standard French pattern for bioinformatics: **generalist biology for two years, computing in L3**. It is the reference for a learner who starts from biology and turns computational late, and a contrast to the double degrees at Paris-Saclay, which mix the two from L1.

## Coverage

| Stage | Structure | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| L1 to L2 | Common life-sciences foundation (units not verified) | [[Biology]], [[Cell Biology]], [[Molecular Biology]], [[Genetics]], [[Biochemistry]] |
| L3 parcours | Biologie-Informatique (BI); Génétique (Magistère européen de génétique); Parcours Interdisciplinaire en Biologie (PIB); Biochimie, biologie intégrative et physiologie | [[Genetics]], [[Physiology]] |
| L3 BI | Multidisciplinary life sciences and computer science, aiming at high-level skills in both; opens masters in bioinformatics, life sciences or applied computer science | [[Bioinformatics]], [[Programming]], [[Algorithms]], [[Sequence Analysis]] |

The degree is a national licence at level 6 (bac+3).

## How to use it

- Use it to set the latest acceptable point for starting bioinformatics: in a French licence, specialization begins in L3.
- The vault should start earlier than this (see [[Curriculum Benchmark]]), but it confirms that L1 and L2 biology depth is not sacrificed for computing.

## Caveats

- The detailed maquette (units and ECTS per semester) was **not verified**; the L1 to L2 mappings are indicative.
- Parcours names and offers change with each accreditation period; check the current catalog (odf.u-paris.fr).
