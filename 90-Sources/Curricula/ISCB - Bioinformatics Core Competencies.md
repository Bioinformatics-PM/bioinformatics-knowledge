# ISCB - Bioinformatics Core Competencies

> [!abstract]
> The ISCB Curriculum Task Force's framework of bioinformatics core competencies, published as Mulder et al. 2018, "The development and application of bioinformatics core competencies to improve bioinformatics training and education", *PLoS Computational Biology* 14(2): e1005772.

## Why this source

Degree programs say *which courses* to take; this framework says *what a trained person must be able to do*. It is the international reference for designing bioinformatics training by outcomes, so the vault can check its syllabus against competencies instead of against one university's course list. Its lead author is at the University of Cape Town and its twelve authors come from the ISCB education community, which balances the institution-specific notes in [[Curriculum Benchmark]].

## How to use it

- After drafting a domain MOC, compare its learning path with the competency list and note any competency with no vault note: that is a backlog item.
- Use it as the outcome layer on top of the course-based benchmark: the programs give order and weight, the competencies give the finish line.
- Cite the paper (not a program) when a design choice in the vault is justified by "what a bioinformatician must be able to do".

## Caveats

- The competency list, its wording and any learner profiles defined in the paper were **not verified** in this pass: read the full text before mapping individual competencies to notes.
- The competencies were refined iteratively; check the ISCB education pages for a version later than 2018 before relying on details.
- It defines outcomes, not a year-by-year plan or credit weights.
