# UC San Diego - BS Bioinformatics

> [!abstract]
> UC San Diego's Bioinformatics major (code BI34): a full lower-division science base (biology, general and organic chemistry, physics, mathematics, programming) followed by an upper division of data structures, algorithms, molecular biology and a named bioinformatics sequence.

## Why this source

The most explicit **named bioinformatics sequence** in the benchmark: Molecular Sequence Analysis, Biological Databases, Applied Genomic Technologies and an advanced bioinformatics laboratory. Its lower division is also the heaviest in chemistry and physics among the computational programs verified here, so it marks the "science-first" end of the spectrum.

Requirements for students admitted in fall 2024. Lower division (LD) is normally years 1 and 2 of a four-year degree; upper division (UD) years 3 and 4.

## How to use it

- Use the four named bioinformatics courses as the skeleton of the vault's L3 bioinformatics syllabus: sequences, then databases, then genomic technologies, then an integrative lab project.
- Note the order: data structures and algorithms (CSE 100, 101) sit in the same upper division as sequence analysis, so they are learned just before or alongside it.

## Caveats

- The URL path still contains "fall18"; the page content verified here states that it applies to students admitted in fall 2024. Requirements vary by admission year.
- Titles of the lower-division courses (BILD, CHEM 6, CSE, MATH, PHYS series) were not verified individually; only their subject area is certain.
- Cross-listed numbers (BIMM, BENG, CSE 181 to 185) name the same courses in different departments.
