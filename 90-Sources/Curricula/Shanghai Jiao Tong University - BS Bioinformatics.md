# Shanghai Jiao Tong University - BS Bioinformatics

> [!abstract]
> SJTU's four-year Bioinformatics major: a year and a half of common "platform" courses shared by all life-science students, a choice of major in the fourth semester, then a bioinformatics core (algorithms, programming, biostatistics, structural and systems biology, genomics, big data) ending with a thesis from semester 7.

## Why this source

A clear example of the **late-branching** model used by Chinese research universities: bioinformatics is a major chosen inside a life-sciences school after common foundations, not a separate entry track. Its course list is also unusually explicit about algorithms (both general and bioinformatics-specific) and about [[Structural Bioinformatics]].

## Coverage

Structure described for the 2019 intake; 160.5 credits to graduate, of which 81.5 credits are platform courses.

A 2023 plan for a "Biotechnology (Bioinformatics Pilot Class)" names mathematics, computer science, biology and chemistry as the main knowledge domains, with biostatistics, structural bioinformatics and computational chemical biology as branches.

## How to use it

- Use its bioinformatics core as a checklist for the vault's L3: every item above has a home in the [[Bioinformatics]] MOC.
- Note that algorithms appear twice, as general computer science and as "principles of algorithms in bioinformatics": the vault does the same by keeping [[Algorithms]] in Computer Science and linking to it from bioinformatics notes.

## Caveats

- The semester of each core course was not verified; only the platform period, the choice of major in semester 4 and the thesis from semester 7 are.
- Platform course content (mathematics, physics, chemistry credits) was not verified.
- Sources are an English summary page and a "curriculum topology" PDF on the school's site; the program has since evolved (2023 pilot class), so check the latest plan.
