# Sorbonne Université - Licence Sciences de la Vie

> [!abstract]
> Sorbonne Université's generalist life-sciences licence: a pluridisciplinary first year, then L2 and L3 in life sciences, with two L3 majors, "De la molécule à l'organisme" (MOrga) and "Organismes, Biodiversité, Écologie, Évolution" (OBEE).

## Why this source

It represents the **generalist French biology licence** with no bioinformatics track: the baseline that bioinformatics-oriented programs modify. Its stated goal, understanding the tree of life and how organisms function from the molecular scale to integrated systems, is a useful scope statement for the vault's [[Biology]] domain.

## Coverage

| Stage | Structure | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Pluridisciplinary first year (content not verified) | [[Biology]], [[Chemistry]], [[Mathematics]] |
| L2 to L3 | Life-sciences major; in monodisciplinary tracks, the major is combined with complementary teaching units | [[Cell Biology]], [[Molecular Biology]], [[Genetics]] |
| L3 major MOrga | From the molecule to the organism | [[Molecular Biology]], [[Biochemistry]], [[Physiology]] |
| L3 major OBEE | Organisms, biodiversity, ecology, evolution | [[Evolution]], [[Microbial Ecology\|Ecology]], [[Phylogenetics]] |

## How to use it

- Use the MOrga / OBEE split to check that the vault's biology covers both molecular and organismal/evolutionary biology: bioinformatics needs [[Evolution]] and [[Phylogenetics]] as much as molecular biology.
- A Sorbonne student reaching bioinformatics does so through a combination with computer science or through a master; the vault's Lab projects play that bridging role.

## Caveats

- Only the overall structure was verified; units, ECTS and the content of the pluridisciplinary L1 were not. Mappings are indicative.
- A combination of the life-sciences major with computer science (double major) may exist but was not verified.
