# Université Paris-Saclay - Licence Double Diplôme Mathématiques Sciences de la Vie

> [!abstract]
> A selective three-year double licence (two diplomas: mathematics and life sciences) on the Orsay campus, with a biology block and a mathematics block every year, a supervised bio-mathematics project in the first year, and a choice of three L3 paths.

## Why this source

The French model of an **intensive double degree** at the mathematics and biology interface. It shows that a French L1 can already carry full biology and full mathematics in parallel, and that the interface is built by projects (the semester 2 bio-mathematics project) rather than by dedicated interface courses. This is the path closest to the vault's [[Mathematics]] plus [[Biology]] backbone.

## How to use it

- Use it as a benchmark for **intensity**: a double licence adds roughly a third more work per year; the vault's L1 can legitimately combine full biology and full mathematics.
- Copy the project idea: one small bio-mathematics modeling project in L1, before formal courses on [[Differential Equations]] or [[Statistical Inference]] are complete.

## Caveats

- The detailed list of teaching units (UE) and their ECTS was **not verified**; only the block structure, the L1 project and the L3 paths were. Vault mappings in the L1 to L3 rows are indicative.
- Admission requires a strong level in mathematics, life sciences and chemistry at the end of secondary school.
- Program pages change every academic year; check the current maquette.
