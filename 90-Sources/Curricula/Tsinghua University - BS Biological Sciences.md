# Tsinghua University - BS Biological Sciences

> [!abstract]
> The four-year undergraduate training plan (培养方案) of Tsinghua's School of Life Sciences: university general education, a 16-credit mathematics block, natural-science foundations, a molecular core taught partly in English, and restricted electives that include biostatistics and an introduction to bioinformatics.

## Why this source

A top Chinese biology program with **credits per course** published, useful to calibrate the mathematics load of a biology degree (calculus, linear algebra, probability and statistics all required) and to see where bioinformatics sits in a non-computational program: a 2-credit restricted elective.

## Coverage

Credits as published in recent training plans (the total varies by cohort, see Caveats).

| Block | Courses (credits) | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics (16) | Calculus B(1) (5); Calculus B(2) (4); Linear Algebra (4); Probability and Mathematical Statistics (3) | [[Calculus]], [[Linear Algebra]], [[Probability]], [[Statistical Inference]] |
| Natural-science foundations | university chemistry, organic chemistry, university physics and a computing course (credits not verified) | [[General Chemistry]], [[Organic Chemistry]], [[Physics]], [[Programming]] |
| Major required | Biochemistry (1) and (2), in English (3 + 3); Biochemistry laboratory (4); Cell Biology (4); Molecular Biology, in English (3); Genetics, in English (3); Physiology (3) | [[Biochemistry]], [[Cell Biology]], [[Molecular Biology]], [[Genetics]], [[Physiology]] |
| Major restricted electives | Biophysics (3); Microbiology, in English (3); Fundamentals of Biostatistics (3); Introduction to Bioinformatics (2) | [[Biophysics]], [[Microbiology]], [[Statistical Inference]], [[Bioinformatics]] |

Published totals: 156 credits for the 2022 cohort (47 general education, 92 major-related, 17 practice); 153 credits in the 2024 plan of Zhili College (致理书院).

## How to use it

- Use the mathematics block as the minimum for any biology-based path: two semesters of calculus, one of linear algebra and one of probability and statistics.
- Read it with [[Shanghai Jiao Tong University - BS Bioinformatics]] to see the difference between a biology major with one bioinformatics elective and a full bioinformatics major.

## Caveats

- The plans are in Chinese; course names are translated. Semester placement was not verified.
- Several cohort-specific plans exist (2022 to 2026, main school and Zhili College); credits above may differ slightly between them.
- Credits for chemistry, physics and computing courses were not verified.
