# MIT - Course 7 Biology

> [!abstract]
> MIT's Bachelor of Science in Biology: a compact molecular core (genetics, biochemistry, cell biology), organic chemistry and physical chemistry, two laboratory subjects and three restricted electives, on top of MIT's common science core.

## Why this source

It is the reference point for "what a molecular biology major requires, and nothing more". Read next to [[MIT - Course 6-7 Computer Science and Molecular Biology]], it isolates the biology and chemistry that stay non-negotiable even when a program turns computational: [[Genetics]], [[Biochemistry]], [[Cell Biology]] and [[Organic Chemistry]].

## Coverage

As for all MIT majors, the chart lists requirements without semester placement; the General Institute Requirements (GIR) science core comes first.

| Block | Subjects (units) | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| GIR science core (first) | Calculus 18.01, 18.02; Physics 8.01, 8.02; Chemistry 5.111, 5.112 or 3.091; Biology 7.012, 7.014, 7.015 or 7.016 (these cover biochemistry, genetics, molecular and cell biology) | [[Calculus]], [[Physics]], [[General Chemistry]], [[Molecular Biology]] |
| Laboratory | 7.002 Fundamentals of Experimental Molecular Biology (6); 7.003[J] Applied Molecular Biology Laboratory (12, CI-M) | [[Molecular Biology]] |
| Molecular core | 7.03 Genetics (12); 7.05 General Biochemistry (12) or 5.07[J]; 7.06 Cell Biology (12) | [[Genetics]], [[Biochemistry]], [[Cell Biology]] |
| Chemistry | 5.12 Organic Chemistry I (12); 5.601 and 5.602 Thermodynamics I, Thermodynamics II and Kinetics (12) or 20.110[J] | [[Organic Chemistry]], [[Thermodynamics]], [[Reaction Kinetics\|Chemical Kinetics]] |
| Communication | 7.19 Communication in Experimental Biology (12, CI-M) | [[Scientific Writing]] |
| Electives | three 12-unit subjects from the restricted elective list (36) | depends on choice |

The departmental program totals 126 units, 36 of which also count toward the GIRs.

## How to use it

- Use the molecular core as the minimum biology depth of the vault's L2: every bioinformatics program in [[Curriculum Benchmark]] with a verified list keeps genetics and biochemistry.
- Note what is absent: no required programming, statistics or linear algebra. A pure biology major leaves the whole quantitative side to electives, which is exactly the gap this vault fills.

## Caveats

- No year-by-year plan is published; only the GIR block is known to come first.
- The restricted elective list was not verified here.
