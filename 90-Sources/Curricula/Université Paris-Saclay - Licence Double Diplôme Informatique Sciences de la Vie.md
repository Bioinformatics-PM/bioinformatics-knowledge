# Université Paris-Saclay - Licence Double Diplôme Informatique Sciences de la Vie

> [!abstract]
> A selective three-year double licence in computer science and life sciences (80 ECTS per year, including a 20 ECTS research-oriented complementary block), taught on the Évry and Versailles sites of Université Paris-Saclay, with an introduction to bioinformatics already in L1.

## Why this source

The French program closest to the vault's purpose: it trains biology and computer science **together from the first semester**, and puts an introduction to bioinformatics in L1 next to Python programming. It is the French counterpart of [[Carnegie Mellon University - BS Computational Biology]] for early exposure.

## Coverage

Verified L1 structure (Évry site):

| L1 block | Teaching units | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Biology (15.5 ECTS) | Chemistry-Biology: at the origins of life; Introduction to bioinformatics ("Introduction bio-informatique"); Biology 1: unity, diversity and evolution of living things; Biology 2: from molecule to organism | [[General Chemistry]], [[Bioinformatics]], [[Evolution]], [[Cell Biology]], [[Molecular Biology]] |
| Support disciplines (16.5 ECTS) | Python programming; General physics | [[Programming]], [[Physics]] |
| Transverse skills (12.5 ECTS) | not detailed | [[Scientific Practice]] |
| Complementary block (20 ECTS per year) | research-based | [[Scientific Method]] |

L2 and L3 units were not verified. At Évry, the life-sciences licence also has an L3 parcours "Génomique, biologie et informatique", which lists an artificial-intelligence opening unit.

## How to use it

- Copy its L1 mix for the vault's first stage: introductory biology from molecule to organism and evolution, Python, a first look at bioinformatics, and general physics and chemistry as support.
- Use it to justify putting a light [[Sequence Analysis]] introduction in L1, before algorithms are formally taught.

## Caveats

- The L1 block totals listed here do not add up to 80 ECTS; the computer science block of L1 was not in the verified material.
- The two sites (Évry, Versailles) may differ in details; check the site-specific pages.
- Unit titles are the English translations given by the program page; only L1 was verified.
