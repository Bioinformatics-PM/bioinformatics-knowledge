# ETH Zurich - BSc Biology

> [!abstract]
> ETH Zurich's three-year, 180 ECTS Bachelor in Biology, redesigned in fall 2020 around evolution as a common thread: two fixed years of natural-science foundations with laboratory practicals every semester, then a free third year of block and concept courses.

## Why this source

The only benchmark program with a **verified ECTS breakdown per subject and per year**, which makes it the reference for the weight of chemistry, physics and mathematics in a European biology bachelor. It also places systems biology and a bioinformatics practical in year 2, and it feeds a dedicated Master in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics (see below).

**Year 3**: block courses and concept courses chosen freely according to the intended Master specialization. Master majors include Systems Biology, Molecular and Structural Biology, Biochemistry and Ecology and Evolution.

**Master target**: the Master in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics (ETH Zurich with the Universities of Zurich and Basel, 120 ECTS) has one compulsory course, core courses in four areas (bioinformatics, biophysics, biosystems, big data), computer science methods, lab rotations and an optional industrial internship.

## How to use it

- Take its ratios as the European default: over years 1 and 2, chemistry (lectures and practicals) weighs 30 of 116 ECTS (about a quarter), mathematics and statistics 17, physics 6, computer science 4 plus a 2 ECTS bioinformatics practical.
- Copy the idea of a thread: ETH organizes biology along evolution; the vault can organize bioinformatics along the flow of genetic information.

## Caveats

- The official ETH pages describe the structure; the per-course ECTS tables above come from the Swiss career-guidance portal berufsberatung.ch (programme entry for ETH Biology) and may lag behind the current regulations.
- The official page places computer science in the first year, while the ECTS table lists it in year 2; check the current study plan.
- Course titles are translated from German; the second year also has elective modules not listed here.
