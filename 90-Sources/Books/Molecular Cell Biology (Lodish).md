# Molecular Cell Biology (Lodish)

> [!abstract]
> A reference textbook of molecular cell biology with a strong experimental emphasis; the 4th edition (2000) is free on NCBI Bookshelf.

## Why this source

The main alternative to [[Molecular Biology of the Cell (Alberts)]]. It leans more on molecular genetics, biochemistry and the experiments behind each result, which helps when a concept note needs the "how do we know" angle. Having two free reference texts lets you cross-check claims.

## How to use it

- Use as a second reference next to Alberts, especially for gene regulation and experimental techniques.
- L2 to L3: read the experimental figures to write the "evidence" parts of concept notes.

## Caveats

- The free edition is the 4th (2000). The current edition is the 9th (2021), with a different author team; methods and genomics content is dated in the free edition.
- NCBI Bookshelf may only allow search, not browsing, for this title.
- Chapter titles and numbers were not verified in this pass.
