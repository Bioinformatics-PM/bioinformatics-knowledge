# Principles of Population Genetics (Hartl)

> [!abstract]
> The standard textbook of population genetics, balancing mathematical models with observation, for undergraduate and graduate students.

## Why this source

Develops the models of population genetics step by step, with a summary and problems at the end of each chapter. It gives the quantitative core behind variant analysis, GWAS, molecular evolution and coalescent-based methods.

## How to use it

- L2: Hardy-Weinberg, drift and selection chapters, with the problems.
- L3 to M1: linkage disequilibrium, molecular population genetics and coalescent material.
- For a shorter entry, the same author's *A Primer of Population Genetics and Genomics* (OUP) exists.

## Caveats

- Paid; the 4th edition is the current one listed by OUP (hardcover and e-book).
- Year: catalog records give 2006 for the 4th edition.
- Assumes basic calculus and probability.
