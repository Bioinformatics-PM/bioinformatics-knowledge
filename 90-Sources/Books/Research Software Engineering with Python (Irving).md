# Research Software Engineering with Python (Irving)

> [!abstract]
> A practical book on building research software in Python with the shell, Git, Make and related tools, free online under a Creative Commons license.

## Why this source

Written by experienced instructors from the Carpentries community, it covers what graduate-level researchers need to automate workflows, collaborate, make results trustworthy and publish their work. It assumes only basic Python and includes over a hundred exercises and a glossary of more than two hundred terms. All royalties go to The Carpentries.

## How to use it

- L1 to L2: follow it with a Lab repository open, applying each chapter (tests, packaging, CI) to your own code, in the order of Stage 1 and 2 of [[Software Engineering]].
- Pair it with [[MIT - The Missing Semester of Your CS Education]] for the shell and Git.

## Caveats

- Python-centric; tools evolve quickly (packaging standards in particular), so check current documentation.
- The print book (CRC Press) is paid; the online version is free.
