# Linear Algebra Done Right (Axler)

> [!abstract]
> A proof-based textbook for a second course in linear algebra; the 4th edition (2024) is open access.

## Why this source

The clearest route to understanding linear algebra structurally (vector spaces and linear maps rather than matrix recipes). The 4th edition expands the singular value decomposition, which underlies PCA and many genomics methods, and is free under a Creative Commons licence.

## How to use it

- L2: after a computational first course ([[Introduction to Linear Algebra (Strang)]]), read the early chapters for proofs.
- L3: eigenvalues, inner product spaces and SVD, directly used by [[Principal Component Analysis]].
- The author's site (linear.axler.net) hosts the PDF and extra material.

## Caveats

- Aimed at math majors; determinants come late and there are few numerical applications.
- Only chapter 9 was verified by number; other chapter grouping is by topic.
