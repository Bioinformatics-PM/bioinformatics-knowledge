# Janeway's Immunobiology (Murphy)

> [!abstract]
> The reference textbook of immunology for advanced undergraduates, graduate and medical students, now in its 10th edition.

## Why this source

Founded by Charles Janeway, it presents immunology from one consistent viewpoint: the host's interaction with an environment full of potentially harmful microbes. It goes from innate immunity and antigen recognition to lymphocyte development, adaptive responses and immune disorders, with the molecular detail needed for immune repertoire sequencing, HLA typing and neoantigen prediction.

## How to use it

- L2: after the immune chapters of [[Anatomy and Physiology 2e (OpenStax)]], read the innate immunity and antigen recognition parts.
- L3: read the receptor diversity and antigen presentation parts for Stage 3 of [[Physiology]], and as background for [[Neoantigen Prediction]] in [[Drug Discovery]].

## Caveats

- The current (10th) edition is paid. The 5th edition (2001, Garland Science, as *Immunobiology: The Immune System in Health and Disease*) is on NCBI Bookshelf (NBK10757), searchable but not browsable, hence access "partial"; it is outdated on many points.
- Earlier editions (up to the 9th, 2016) were published by Garland Science.
