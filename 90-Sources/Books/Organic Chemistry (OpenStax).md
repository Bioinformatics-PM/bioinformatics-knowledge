# Organic Chemistry (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> McMurry's bestselling organic chemistry textbook, 10th edition, released free by OpenStax in 2023.

## Why this source

A full commercial-grade organic chemistry text made free by its author, with more than 1800 problems. Its closing biomolecule chapters connect functional-group chemistry to amino acids, nucleic acids and metabolism.

## Coverage

Two-semester course following a functional-group approach.

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chapters 1 to 4 | Structure and bonding, acids and bases, first compound classes (titles not verified) | [[Covalent Bond]], [[Functional Group]] |
| Ch. 5 Stereochemistry at Tetrahedral Centers | Chirality, enantiomers | [[Stereochemistry]], [[Chirality]] |
| Middle chapters | Reactions organized by functional group | [[Reaction Mechanism]] |
| Ch. 26 Biomolecules: Amino Acids, Peptides, and Proteins | Amino acid chemistry, peptide bond | [[Amino Acid]], [[Peptide Bond]], [[Protein]] |
| Ch. 28 Biomolecules: Nucleic Acids | Nucleotides, DNA and RNA chemistry | [[Nucleotide]], [[DNA]], [[RNA]] |
| Ch. 29 The Organic Chemistry of Metabolic Pathways | Mechanisms of metabolic reactions | [[Metabolism]] |

## How to use it

- L1: structure, bonding and stereochemistry chapters.
- L2: the biomolecule chapters (26, 28, 29) as a chemistry-side complement to biochemistry notes.
- The mechanism chapters in the middle are optional for this curriculum.

## Caveats

- Only chapters 5, 26, 28 and 29 were verified by number.
- Instructor resources (solutions manual, test bank) require an instructor account.
