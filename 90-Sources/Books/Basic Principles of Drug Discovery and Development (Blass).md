# Basic Principles of Drug Discovery and Development (Blass)

> [!abstract]
> A textbook covering the whole drug pipeline, from target selection and screening through medicinal chemistry and pharmacokinetics to clinical trials and regulation.

## Why this source

Most drug discovery texts are written for chemists or for pharmacologists; this one follows a program end to end and explains how each stage hands over to the next. That makes it the reference book for the [[Drug Discovery]] subdomain, where a bioinformatician needs to understand the whole value chain before specializing in targets, screening data or clinical biomarkers.

## How to use it

- L3: read [[Hughes 2011 - Principles of Early Drug Discovery]] first for the map, then use this book chapter by chapter along the [[Drug Discovery]] learning path.
- M1: combine the clinical and regulatory parts with [[MIT 15.136J - Principles and Practice of Drug Development]].

## Caveats

- Paid textbook; check library or ScienceDirect access.
- The 2nd edition dates from 2021 (the first appeared in 2015); fast-moving areas such as AI-driven design and new modalities may be thin.
