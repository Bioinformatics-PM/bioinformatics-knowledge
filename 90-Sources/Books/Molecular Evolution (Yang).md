# Molecular Evolution (Yang)

> [!abstract]
> *Molecular Evolution: A Statistical Approach*, the reference on statistical methods and algorithms for comparative analysis of genetic sequences.

## Why this source

Authoritative, up-to-date coverage of models of sequence evolution, likelihood and Bayesian phylogenetics, and phylogenetic tests for selection, by the author of PAML. Emphasizes conceptual understanding with worked numerical examples; web support includes data sets, C and R programs and a probability primer.

## Coverage

A major update of the author's *Computational Molecular Evolution* (2006). Chapter titles not verified here.

## How to use it

- L3: substitution models and likelihood chapters, after [[Inferring Phylogenies (Felsenstein)]] or alongside it.
- M1: Bayesian, coalescent and selection chapters; use the end-of-chapter problems.

## Caveats

- Paid; graduate level. Needs solid probability and calculus.
- The clock-dating row is inferred from the book's scope (molecular evolution and phylogeography) rather than a verified chapter title.
