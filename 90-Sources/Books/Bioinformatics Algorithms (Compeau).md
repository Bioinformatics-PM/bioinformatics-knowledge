# Bioinformatics Algorithms (Compeau)

> [!abstract]
> *Bioinformatics Algorithms: An Active Learning Approach*, the MOOC-born textbook in which each chapter starts from a biological question and builds the algorithms to answer it.

## Why this source

The best-structured bridge between biology and algorithms for this curriculum. Exercises are embedded in the text and automated coding challenges are on Rosalind, so every concept can be implemented and checked. Free lecture videos cover the whole book.

## Coverage

Chapters are named by their biological question. Titles below are verified; chapter numbers are not.

## How to use it

- L2: replication origin, assembly and alignment chapters, solving the matching Rosalind problems.
- L3: phylogeny and pattern-matching chapters, then the later chapters on probabilistic methods.
- Watch the free lecture videos on the site before each chapter.

## Caveats

- Only several chapters are free on the website; the full book is paid (print).
- Chapter titles are verified, but not their numbering or the full list.
