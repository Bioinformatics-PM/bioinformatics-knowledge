# Introductory Statistics (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free textbook for a one-semester introductory statistics course, 2nd edition (2023), assuming only intermediate algebra.

## Why this source

A gentle, example-driven first course with collaborative exercises and statistics labs built on real data. It establishes the vocabulary (sampling, distributions, tests, regression) before the calculus-based treatment in [[Introduction to Probability (Blitzstein)]].

## How to use it

- L1: chapters 1 to 9 in order, then regression and chi-square.
- Use it to write the L1 sections of statistics notes; use the reference texts for L2 and beyond.

## Caveats

- Non-calculus treatment; no likelihood or Bayesian methods.
- Based on the earlier *Collaborative Statistics* by the same authors. The 1st edition (without "2e") also exists on OpenStax; chapter numbering may differ slightly.
