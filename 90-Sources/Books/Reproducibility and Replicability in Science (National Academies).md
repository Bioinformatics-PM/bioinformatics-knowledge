# Reproducibility and Replicability in Science (National Academies)

> [!abstract]
> The US National Academies' consensus report defining reproducibility and replicability, reviewing the evidence on both, and recommending how to improve them.

## Why this source

The two words are used in contradictory ways across fields. This report fixes the definitions: reproducibility means obtaining consistent results with the same data, code and conditions of analysis; replicability means obtaining consistent results across studies that answer the same question with their own data. It then examines what causes failures of each and gives recommendations to researchers, institutions, journals and funders. It is the authoritative reference for the vocabulary of the [[Reproducibility]] subdomain.
