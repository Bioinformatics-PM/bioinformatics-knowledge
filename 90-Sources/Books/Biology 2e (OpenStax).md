# Biology 2e (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free, openly licensed textbook for a two-semester biology course for science majors, taught through an evolutionary lens.

## Why this source

The best free L1 entry point to the whole of biology: complete, peer-reviewed, current, with review questions in every chapter. It gives the first pass that the reference texts ([[Molecular Biology of the Cell (Alberts)]]) then deepen.

## Coverage

47 chapters in 8 units: The Chemistry of Life, The Cell, Genetics, Evolutionary Processes, Biological Diversity, Plant Structure and Function, Animal Structure and Function, Ecology.

## How to use it

- L1 core: Units 1 to 4 in order. Units 5 to 8 (diversity, plants, animals, ecology) are optional for a bioinformatics track.
- Do the end-of-chapter review questions before marking a concept at mastery 2.

## Caveats

- Introductory level: switch to the reference texts for L2 to L3 mechanisms.
- Chapter numbers in Units 1, 2 and 4 were not individually verified; cite by unit or check the online table of contents.
