# Intermolecular and Surface Forces (Israelachvili)

> [!abstract]
> The reference monograph on the forces between molecules and between surfaces: charge, dipole, induced and dispersion interactions, hydrogen bonding and hydrophobic interactions, from simple liquids to colloidal, polymeric and biological systems.

## Why this source

- Treats every intermolecular interaction with the same tools (potential energy as a function of distance), which makes their strengths and ranges directly comparable.
- Goes from two molecules in vacuum to molecules in a solvent and to surfaces and membranes, the situations met in biology.
