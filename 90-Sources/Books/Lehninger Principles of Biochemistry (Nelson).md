# Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry (Nelson)

> [!abstract]
> The standard current biochemistry textbook, organized around fundamental principles; 8th edition (2021).

## Why this source

The most widely used up-to-date biochemistry text. The 8th edition was trimmed to emphasize core principles, with shorter chapters. Use it when the free [[Biochemistry (Berg)]] 5th edition is too dated.

Coverage is described by topic; chapter numbers are not verified here.

## How to use it

- L1 to L2: structure and catalysis part first, then metabolism.
- Cite it for current facts that changed since the free NCBI editions.
