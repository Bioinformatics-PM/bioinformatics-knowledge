# Biochemistry (Berg)

> [!abstract]
> "Stryer", a reference biochemistry textbook; the 5th edition (2002) is free on NCBI Bookshelf.

## Why this source

A clear, structure-first reference for proteins, enzymes and metabolism. The 5th edition's authors explicitly aimed at biochemistry that "may be explored through computers", which fits a bioinformatics curriculum.

## How to use it

- L1 to L2: protein structure and enzyme kinetics chapters back the [[Protein]] and [[Enzyme]] notes.
- Pair with [[Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry (Nelson)]] (paid, current) when a newer treatment is needed.

## Caveats

- By agreement with the publisher, the Bookshelf edition is accessible through search but cannot be browsed.
- Later editions (with Gregory J. Gatto Jr. as co-author) exist and are paid.
- Chapter titles not verified in this pass.
