# Microbiology (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free textbook for a one-semester microbiology course, produced with the American Society for Microbiology Press.

## Why this source

Free, aligned with the ASM curriculum guidelines, and useful for the microbial side of bioinformatics: bacterial metabolism, microbial genetics and its applications (the ground for metagenomics and pathogen genomics).

## How to use it

- L1: chapters 8, 11 and 12 are the core for this curriculum.
- The disease chapters are optional context for pathogen genomics projects.

## Caveats

- Written for non-majors with an allied-health focus; molecular depth is lower than a majors text.
- Only chapters 8, 11 and 12 were verified by number.
