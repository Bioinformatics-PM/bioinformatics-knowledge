# The Cell (Cooper)

> [!abstract]
> *The Cell: A Molecular Approach*, a concise single-author cell biology textbook; the 2nd edition (2000) is free on NCBI Bookshelf.

## Why this source

Shorter and more linear than Alberts or Lodish, written by a single author. Good for a first L1 pass through cell and molecular biology before moving to the reference texts.

## Coverage

The book is organized in parts that include an overview of cells and cell research, the chemistry of cells, and the fundamentals of molecular biology, followed by cell structure and regulation. Chapter-level detail not verified here.

## How to use it

- L1: read the molecular biology parts as a compact alternative to Alberts.
- Switch to [[Molecular Biology of the Cell (Alberts)]] when a note needs L2 to L3 depth.

## Caveats

- By agreement with the publisher, the Bookshelf edition is accessible through the search feature but cannot be browsed.
- The free edition is the 2nd (2000); later editions exist and are paid.
- Part and chapter titles were only partly verified; the table groups content by topic.
