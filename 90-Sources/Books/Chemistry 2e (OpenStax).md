# Chemistry 2e (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free textbook covering the scope and sequence of a two-semester general chemistry course.

## Why this source

Free, complete general chemistry with worked examples and exercises. Covers everything a bioinformatician needs from chemistry at L1: bonding, energetics, equilibria, acids and bases, kinetics.

## How to use it

- L1: chapters 1 to 7, then 12 to 14. The thermodynamics chapter supports [[Gibbs Free Energy]] in biochemistry.
- For biomolecules go on to [[Organic Chemistry (OpenStax)]].

## Caveats

- General chemistry only; biochemical applications are limited.
- Only the chapters listed with numbers were verified by number.
