# Molecular Biology of the Cell (Alberts)

> [!abstract]
> The reference textbook of cell and molecular biology; the 4th edition (2002) is free on NCBI Bookshelf.

## Why this source

The classic in-depth reference for cell biology, widely used from first-year university courses to the laboratory bench. It builds a coherent framework from molecules to cells, which makes it the default citation for most biology concept notes in this vault. The free 4th edition is enough for L1 to L3 fundamentals.

## How to use it

- L1: read the genetic-mechanisms chapters first, alongside [[Biology 2e (OpenStax)]] for a gentler pass.
- L2 to L3: use it as the reference to check and deepen concept notes (replication, gene expression, regulation).
- Use the Bookshelf search box to jump to a topic; cite chapter and section in footnotes once checked on the page.

## Caveats

- The free edition is the 4th (2002). The current edition is the 7th (W. W. Norton), with an updated author list; genomics, RNA biology and methods have moved a lot since 2002.
- NCBI Bookshelf access to some publisher textbooks is limited to search rather than browsing; if the table of contents does not browse, search for the topic.
- Chapter titles and numbers were not verified in this pass; verify on the Bookshelf page before citing a chapter.
