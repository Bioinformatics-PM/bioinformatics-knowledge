# On Being a Scientist (National Academies)

> [!abstract]
> A short guide to the responsible conduct of research from the US National Academies, free online, with discussion cases.

## Why this source

It covers the norms every researcher is expected to know, in academic, industrial and government settings and across all disciplines, in fewer than a hundred pages. The third edition (2009) updates the 1989 and 1995 editions. Its case scenarios make it practical: each topic ends with a situation to discuss rather than a rule to memorize.

## How to use it

- L2: read it in one or two sittings for items 1 to 5 of [[Research Ethics]]; discuss two case scenarios in writing.
- Reread the data and authorship parts before your first collaborative project or publication.

## Caveats

- US perspective and 2009 context: data sharing, preprints and research software norms have evolved; complement with [[The Turing Way]] for computational practice.
