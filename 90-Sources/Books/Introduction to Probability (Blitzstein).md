# Introduction to Probability (Blitzstein)

> [!abstract]
> The textbook of Harvard's Stat 110 probability course, 2nd edition (2019), with a free online version.

## Why this source

Probability taught through stories, strategies and many worked problems, from Harvard's celebrated Stat 110 lectures. It builds the intuition for random variables and conditioning that every statistical and probabilistic bioinformatics method (HMMs, Bayesian phylogenetics, likelihood) relies on.

## Coverage

Coverage by topic, following the scope of a first calculus-based probability course (chapter list not verified here).

## How to use it

- L2: foundations and random variables, doing the exercises marked with solutions.
- L3: joint distributions, limit theorems and Markov chains, before [[Hidden Markov Model]] notes.
- Supplements (selected solutions, practice problems, handouts, lecture videos) are on the Stat 110 site: https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/
