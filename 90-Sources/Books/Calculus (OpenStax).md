# Calculus (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free three-volume textbook for single- and multivariable calculus, derived from Gilbert Strang's calculus text.

## Why this source

Free, complete and exercise-rich, with answers to odd-numbered problems. Covers all the calculus needed for probability, statistics, differential-equation models and optimization in machine learning.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Volume 1 | Functions, limits, derivatives, integration | [[Function]], [[Limit]], [[Derivative]], [[Integral]] |
| Volume 2 | Techniques and applications of integration, introduction to differential equations, sequences and series, power series, parametric equations and polar coordinates | [[Integral]], [[Ordinary Differential Equation]], [[Infinite Series\|Series]], [[Taylor Series]] |
| Volume 3 | Parametric and polar, vectors, functions of several variables, multiple integration, second-order differential equations | [[Vector]], [[Partial Derivative]], [[Gradient]], [[Multiple Integral]] |

## How to use it

- L1: Volume 1 in full; Volume 2 integration and series.
- L2: Volume 2 differential equations, then Volume 3 (partial derivatives and gradients are needed for optimization and likelihood).
- Volumes 2 and 3 have their own pages on openstax.org (`calculus-volume-2`, `calculus-volume-3`).

## Caveats

- Computational focus; for proofs and rigor, pair with an analysis course.
- Coverage is given per volume; individual chapter titles not listed here.
