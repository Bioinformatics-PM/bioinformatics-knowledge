# Evolution (Futuyma)

> [!abstract]
> The standard undergraduate evolutionary biology textbook, 5th edition (2023), with updated coverage of evolutionary genetics and genomics.

## Why this source

Presents evolution as a living science that tests theory against data, from genomes to ecological communities. It is the conceptual frame for everything phylogenetic and population-genetic in bioinformatics.

## Coverage

The book addresses the history of evolution, evolutionary processes and adaptation across levels of organization. Chapter-level detail not verified here.

## How to use it

- L2: after [[Biology 2e (OpenStax)]] Unit 4, read the processes and phylogenetics parts.
- L3: the genetics and genomics chapters, alongside [[Principles of Population Genetics (Hartl)]] for the mathematics.
- Student resources are on Oxford Learning Link.
