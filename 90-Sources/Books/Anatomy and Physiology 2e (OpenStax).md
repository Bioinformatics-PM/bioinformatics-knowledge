# Anatomy and Physiology 2e (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free, peer-reviewed textbook for a two-semester human anatomy and physiology course, organized by body system.

## Why this source

It gives a biology or bioinformatics student the human physiology that molecular courses skip: tissues, organ systems, blood, the nervous and endocrine systems, and the immune system. It is free in web view and PDF under a CC BY 4.0 license, and the web version links to histology images and interactive diagrams. The 2e revision redrew many illustrations and focused on inclusive instruction.

## How to use it

- L1: read the chapters matching Stage 1 of [[Physiology]] in order; answer the end-of-chapter review questions.
- L2: read the immune system chapter before moving to [[Janeway's Immunobiology (Murphy)]] for Stage 3.

## Caveats

- Written for allied health majors: strong on anatomy, light on molecular mechanisms and data.
