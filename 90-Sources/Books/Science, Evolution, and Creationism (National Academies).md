# Science, Evolution, and Creationism (National Academies)

> [!abstract]
> A short book by the US National Academy of Sciences that explains the nature of science, summarizes the evidence for biological evolution, and clarifies what scientists mean by fact, hypothesis, law and theory.

## Why this source

It is written for teachers, students and the public by the main US scientific academy, so it gives the authoritative, plain-language version of the vocabulary that public debates confuse. Its formal definition of a theory, "a comprehensive explanation of some aspect of nature that is supported by a vast body of evidence", answers the "just a theory" objection directly.

## How to use it

- L1: read the opening part on the nature of science alongside [[Scientific Theory]]; it takes under an hour.
- Use the evidence part as a map before the detailed treatment in [[Evolution (Futuyma)]].

## Caveats

- Written for a US public-education context, including legal and policy questions about teaching creationism.
- An overview, not a textbook: it gives no derivations or quantitative methods.
- Verified in this pass: title, issuing academy, publisher, year (published January 2008), free online reading at the URL above, and the wording of its formal definition of theory. The joint role of the Institute of Medicine was not checked.
