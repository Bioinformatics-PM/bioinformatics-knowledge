# Molecular Driving Forces (Dill)

> [!abstract]
> The standard introduction to statistical thermodynamics for chemists and biologists: from counting and the Boltzmann law to binding, cooperativity, electrostatics, water and polymers.

## Why this source

It derives thermodynamics from simple molecular models with chemical and biological examples, at a level between a general physics text and a graduate statistical mechanics course. It is the book the [[Statistical Physics]] MOC planned for the statistical side of [[Thermodynamics]].

## How to use it

- L2: the Boltzmann-law and partition-function chapters alongside Stage 2 of [[Statistical Physics]].
- L3: ch. 26 and 27 for cooperativity and binding, after [[Two-State Model]] and [[Ising Model]].
