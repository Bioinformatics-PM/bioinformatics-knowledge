# University Physics (OpenStax)

> [!abstract]
> A free three-volume calculus-based physics textbook for two- and three-semester courses.

## Why this source

Free, rigorous, calculus-based physics. For a bioinformatics track the useful parts are mechanics, oscillations, thermodynamics and the modern-physics chapters behind spectroscopy and imaging.

## Coverage

| Part | Content | Vault notes |
|---|---|---|
| Volume 1 | Mechanics, sound, oscillations, waves | [[Mechanics\|Newtonian Mechanics]], [[Conservation of Energy\|Energy]], [[Harmonic Oscillator\|Oscillation]], [[Wave]] |
| Volume 2 | Thermodynamics, electricity and magnetism | [[Thermodynamics]], [[Thermodynamic Entropy\|Entropy]], [[Coulomb's Law\|Electrostatics]], [[Magnetic Field\|Magnetism]] |
| Volume 3, Unit 1 Optics | Nature of light, geometric optics, interference, diffraction | [[Waves and Optics\|Optics]], [[Diffraction]] |
| Volume 3, Unit 2 Modern Physics | Relativity, photons and matter waves, quantum mechanics, atomic structure, condensed matter, nuclear, particle physics | Quantum Mechanics, [[Atom]] |

## How to use it

- L1: Volume 1 (mechanics, energy, oscillations) and the thermodynamics part of Volume 2.
- L2: quantum mechanics and atomic structure in Volume 3, as background for spectroscopy and structural biology.
- Volumes 2 and 3 have their own OpenStax pages (`university-physics-volume-2`, `university-physics-volume-3`).

## Caveats

- Requires calculus: take [[Calculus (OpenStax)]] Volume 1 in parallel.
- No biology applications; see [[Physical Biology of the Cell (Phillips)]] for physics applied to cells.
