# Physical Biology of the Cell (Phillips)

> [!abstract]
> A textbook for a first course in physical biology or biophysics that organizes cell and molecular biology by the physical concepts that unite them.

## Why this source

It teaches quantitative, order-of-magnitude reasoning about cells ("biology by the numbers") and simple physical models of biological phenomena. This is the mindset behind quantitative and systems biology, and a strong complement to the descriptive reference texts.

## How to use it

- L3: chapter 1 early, as a habit of estimation for every biology note.
- M1: work through further chapters with their end-of-chapter problems; a hints document for the problems is on the authors' Caltech group site.

## Caveats

- Paid. Assumes calculus, basic physics and introductory molecular biology.
- Only the chapters listed with numbers were verified; the remaining chapters are not mapped here yet.
- First published by Garland Science; now sold by Routledge (Taylor & Francis).
